## Decisions taken

- **No reading rows.** Spanish has no inherited Chinese readings; cards carry meaning and note only.
- **Register:** neutral Latin American Spanish, tú to the learner, ustedes, never vos / vosotros (es.md §1, es-ko). In glosses, **usted only on lines with 您 or 贵**; tú for 你; no person for stranger/staff lines without a pronoun.
- **Measure words are *clasificadores*** (the roadmap's term), not es-ko's *contador*, which stays Korean-only.
- **Titles:** 14 lesson titles come verbatim from `tools/i18n/es/roadmap.json`; the other 14 are fixed in profile §3.5. **Title 21 adds *saber*** («想, 要, 会, 能: querer, saber y poder») where the roadmap says «querer y poder»: align `roadmap.json` or revert the title.
- **Names fixed in the profile:** course «Chino mandarín»; 6 groups; 31 deck labels («Trampas frecuentes» for the English-traps deck; «X clave» for "Top X"; «Palabras básicas» for Top words); level titles are gender-invariable (Brote, Trotamundos, Estudiante, Hablante, Pro, As, Leyenda).
- **Pinyin is taught from Spanish sounds only:** b d g = Spanish p t k; h = jota; j q x z c r g ü and qu each get a Spanish-spelling warning. All three sound lessons go to w2, so one writer keeps the anchors; w1 copies them into the pinyin cards.
- **Tone marks vs the Spanish tilde** are explained in `pinyin` and `tones`; Spanish keyboards type á / à / ü directly, ā / ǎ through the barra de tonos (app behaviour taken from the vi-zh profile, not re-tested).
- **Prompt pinning:** the subject is always named when the answer has a pronoun; usted → 您; «ya» → final 了; imperfecto → no 了; *saber* → 会; gender: generic masculine in sentences, «amigo/a» in card meanings.
- **Places:** Pekín, Shanghái, Hong Kong, Taiwán; other cities in pinyin. 美国 = Estados Unidos / estadounidense, never América.
- **Numbers follow es-ko:** 1005 (no separator), 10,000 (comma); no decimals; *yuanes*, *mao*.
- **Vocative 服务员 = «¡Mesero!»**; fixed courtesy glosses in profile §3.2.
