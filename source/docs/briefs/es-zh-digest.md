# es-zh digest: what a writer keeps at hand

Mandarin for Spanish speakers. The Chinese (characters, pinyin, answers) is fixed in `tools/halves/target/zh/`. You write the Spanish. Authority: `docs/profiles/es-zh.md` (section numbers below). This page is a summary; where they differ, the profile wins.

## Voice
- Neutral Latin American Spanish. **tú** to the learner, **ustedes** for groups, never *vos / vosotros*. Always **¿ ¡**. Spanish quotations and sound anchors in **« »** (nested « » as es-ko). (§3.1)
- es-ko's neutral words: *celular, boleto, manejar, mesero, jugo, piscina, conversación, reunión, pequeño*; plus *computadora, auto, apartamento, autobús, metro, fideos, de acuerdo* (not *vale*).
- Glosses of Chinese lines: **usted only if the line has 您 or 贵**; tú for 你; ustedes for 你们; no person («Perdón, ¿…?», «…, por favor») for stranger/staff lines without a pronoun. (§3.2)
- **No reading rows.** No English anywhere. No English sound comparisons.

## Fixed terms (§3.3)
*primer / segundo / tercer / cuarto tono, tono neutro, cambio de tono, marca de tono* (never *tilde / acento*), *inicial / final* (feminine), *con soplo / sin soplo*, *carácter, trazo, radical* (never *ideograma*), *simplificado / tradicional*, **clasificador** (never *contador*), *palabra de ubicación, palabra de tiempo, partícula, pregunta de sí o no, pregunta verbo-no-verbo, verbo modal, verbo separable, complemento de resultado / de grado, oración con 把 / 比*. Language: *chino, chino mandarín*; *pinyin* (el, invariable). Tabs **Hoy, Práctica, Repaso, Lecciones, Entrenar, Progreso**; *Comprobar, Mis frases, mazo, tarjeta, prueba rápida, fichas*; the tone keys are **«la barra de tonos»**.

## Reading guide in one breath (§4)
b d g = Spanish p t k; p t k = the same + a puff · h = jota, never silent · g always hard (哥 gē) · j = soft «ch», tongue down, never jota · q = j + puff, never k; **qu = «chü»** · x = flat «s», near «¡shh!», never «ks» or jota · zh ch sh r = tongue curled back; r never «pero» · z = «ts» of «pizza» («pitsa»), c = the same + puff; never [θ], k or s · e = «o» with spread lips (but Spanish e in ie, üe, ei) · -i after z c s zh ch sh r = buzz, **四 sì ≠ «sí»** · ü = «i» with rounded lips; not the diaeresis of «pingüino» · ian = «ien», ui = «uei» («buey»), iu = «iou» · -ng = n of «tengo»; -n ≠ -ng.
Tones: 1 high level (a held note) · 2 rising («¿eh?») · 3 low, dips alone · 4 falls short and firm («¡no!») · neutral short («MA-ma», never «mamá»). Tilde ´ = 2nd tone, not stress; ` = 4th; every syllable has its mark. Questions keep their tones. Keyboard: á é í ó ú, ü and à are typed directly; ā ǎ via the barra de tonos or fichas; turn off autocorrect.

## Bridges and traps (§5)
Easier: no *do*-support; no + verb = 不 / 没 + verb; pro-drop; tú/usted ≈ 你/您; **saber = 会, poder = 能 / 可以**; *estar* + gerundio = 在 + verb; hay / está = 有 / 在; *imperfecto* → no 了; **ya ≈ final 了**, *ya no* ≈ 不…了, *todavía no* ≈ 还没; «¿vas o no vas?» = 去不去; «¿verdad?» ≈ 吧; time first; clock order; partitives.
Harder: **是 = ser + noun only** (adjectives take 很; *estar en* = 在; *estar* + gerundio = 在); *tener años / hambre* ≠ 有; **everything describing a noun goes before it** (*X 的 Y* = «Y de X»); place and means of transport before the verb; 了 ≠ *pretérito*; no agreement; ask with 吗, not intonation; *no* + command = 别; one negation only (*tampoco* = 也不); 和 only joins nouns; *o* = 还是 (question) / 或者 (statement); dates big to small.

## Prompt rules (§8)
- Read `a`, `o`, `x`, `strict` in `target/zh/practice.jsonl` first. The Spanish must lead to `a` and fit nothing else.
- **Name the subject** if the answer has a pronoun (Spanish would drop it).
- Cues: usted → 您 · *pretérito* + counted object → V了 · «ya» → final 了 · *imperfecto* / state → no 了 · *pretérito* negative or *no tengo / no hay* → 没 · counted noun or clock hour → 两 · *y* → 和, ¿…o…? → 还是, statement *o* + «(da igual cuál)» → 或者 · «nosotros (tú y yo)» → 咱们 · *él / ella* written · *estar* + gerundio → 在 · «alguna vez» → 过 · *saber* → 会, *poder* + reason → 能, *¿puedo…?* → 可以 · «me gustaría» → 想, «quiero / tengo que» → 要.
- Gender: card meanings «amigo/a»; sentences, prompts, options: generic masculine; agree when the Chinese marks gender (她, 姐姐) or the speaker is named.
