## The pron

- **Format:** the tone line only (profile §6.1, §6.4).
  - One word per syllable from the tone letter: high · falling · rising · mid · low · falling-breathy · creaky · rising-final.
  - `—` for an English word kept in Hmong text, a name in English spelling or a digit. `hnub Monday` → `high · —`.
- **Reading help:** the §6.3 reading guide (x = s, s ≈ sh, z, c, q, r, w, ee / oo, aspirates, pre-nasalised) is lesson text. `respell.js --aid` prints the rows that apply to a text. Inventing English respellings of RPA syllables was rejected.
- **Parser:** `respell.js` requires `tools/hmong/rpa_check.js` (`syllable`, `segment`, `checkToken`, `loadAllow`) and `coverage.js` (`loadGlossary`). It does not copy the parser.
- **It throws** on illegal, Green and marginal spellings, a bare letter, a digit glued to a word, an unknown capitalised word, and a run-together spelling that is not a glossary or NEW-WORDS word (the `wa` blind spot).
- **Selftest:** every tone letter, all 56 onsets of RPA.md §8 plus the zero onset (asserted against `rpa_check.ONSETS`), the 40 §8 words with tone lines written by hand, the §8 negative tests, names, loans, digits, joined glossary words, and `pattern() === profile.json pronPattern`.
- The file is named `respell.js`, not the profile's `toneline.js`, because mk_briefs.py and repron.js need that name.

## Decisions (full text: docs/briefs/en-hmn-decisions.md)

- **Owner ruling applied:**
  - `native check` rows are usable.
  - WV-only rows are usable only where no better-sourced form exists.
  - Never invent a word, and never guess a tone letter.
  - New words go in NEW-WORDS.tsv only with a source URL.
- **Days:** `hnub Monday` … `hnub Sunday` (PGE, MNC). The numbered days appear only in notes, with the ECM / Wikipedia conflict stated. `hnub so` = day off. Week = `lim tiam`.
- **One million:** `ib plhom` (BSL + BS1011), with the note "also ib lab; Omniglot ib roob". Answers accept all three.
- **Other numbers:**
  - 10,000 = `kaum txhiab` (also `ib vam`); zero = `ntxaiv`;
  - dollar = `daus las` (also `daulas`);
  - numbers are written as words, except clock times `7 sawv ntxov`.
- **Questions:** `puas` + verb, `… los tsis yog?`, final `los`, `… puas tau?`. Question words stay in place. Never `pov`.
- **Final particles, exactly four:** `os`, `los` (taught to produce); `yam`, `ko` (recognition only).
- **Loans:** keep the English word where MDH / ECM / PGE do, with a card note saying so and an ALLOW.txt entry. The gap words are taught only with a source.
- **Religion-neutral wording:** traditional ceremonies are described factually ("many families"), `dab` = spirit, no belief asked of the learner, no unsourced church vocabulary.
- **Kinship:**
  - core terms by speaker's sex, from WHL, GYL and W-KK;
  - no grandparent side claimed beyond `pog`;
  - `niam laus` / `niam hluas`, `niam hlob` / `niam ntxawm` and `npawg` are not taught.
- **Typing:**
  - no `!strict` (tone letters are always strict for hmn);
  - the tone twin goes in the wrong options;
  - answers include the prompted subject pronoun;
  - joined forms are listed only where the glossary spells them differently.
- **Standing rule:** lessons 1–10 take their hits from the tone strand, and the classifier strand opens at lesson 11.

## Known gaps in the guards (told to writers and the reviewer)

- **Lesson prose is not read** by rpa_check or coverage. They read only `{{ex:}}` rows and `<td class="k">` cells. `check.js` covers `<b>` (Hmong only, glossary words, matching tone line in `<i>`).
- **coverage works syllable by syllable,** so a made-up compound of real syllables passes. Only the reviewer catches it.
- **English in a correct mc option or a check answer** is flagged by rpa_check unless the word also appears in English text of the pack (probed with a scratch pack).
- **Hmong inside English fields is read by no guard:** card notes, meanings, `why` lines, hints and mc questions. The reviewer reads these.
- **Green forms and placeholders** (`dlev`, `X loj tshaj Y`) must stay out of `<b>` and `<td class="k">`, or check.js and rpa_check flag them. The writer note says so.
- **The capitalised names Lee, Lo and Pha are also legal RPA syllables.** respell reads them as names (`—`) inside a sentence and as RPA at the start of one, and warns.
