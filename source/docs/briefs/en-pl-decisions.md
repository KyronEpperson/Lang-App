## en-pl stage-1 decisions

1. Four cases drilled: YES. Accusative, instrumental, genitive, and locative, plus the nominative as the base form. Dative and vocative are named once and not drilled.
2. You-form: YES. Learners produce pan / pani plus a third-person verb for every "you" (Pan jest, never Pan jesteś). ty, państwo, panowie, and panie are recognition only. Learners never type a ty answer.
3. accents false: YES. The app folds ą, ę, ó, ć, ń, ś, ź, and ż. ł does not fold. Writers mark strict when a pair differs only by a folded letter. wodą and woda are that kind of pair.
4. One added lesson: YES. traps is last and points at Traps for English speakers. Compliments is pointed at from adjectives. 27 lessons. Roadmap order stays.
5. Careful stress in the aid: YES. Antepenultimate stress stays on the closed list (muzyka, the -ika words, Ameryka, uniwersytet, the four numbers). Past -śmy is ignored. Writers do not hand-fix a pron when the voice sounds penultimate.
6. Banner greetings: YES. Morning and afternoon are both Dzień dobry. Evening and the late-night slot are both Dobry wieczór. Dobranoc stays a lesson item, not the banner.
7. Standing rule: YES. Gender agreement in every lesson, and the four drilled cases once they exist. The regex does not contain dative or vocative.
8. Voicing: YES. The aid devoices a final consonant before a vowel or a sonorant. Kraków voicing is named and not shown.

Pron: as listed. Keep proszę / prosię as the ś / sz contrast. Run a whole line, not word by word. Do not use Chicago. Do not build a card on jabłko. muzyka is the music noun only. Do not hand-edit a pron.

App changes: none needed. accents stays false.

Placeholders: keep. Brand word Polski. Level titles stay the Spanish list.

Go for stage 2: YES
