## Decisions

1. **Reference course ko, IPA from vi.**
   - The siblings with no articles (zh, vi, id, kn) are ko-based, and the profile says so too.
   - The IPA follows vi-en's conventions, with `tools/content/vi/PRON-RULES.md` binding. The profile asks for this, vi has the `final-sounds` lesson to copy, and zh's IPA fails the vi pattern in 68 rows (ceb's fails it in 63).
   - Writers paste from `pron_lookup.json`.
2. **cueTest.**
   - The pattern is 65 frequent Hmong glossary words and phrases (the profile's §7 list plus kuv, koj, nws … li cas, dab tsi, nag hmo, tag kis, tam sim no), case-folded on the first letter and matched on word boundaries.
   - Tested against the 2,496 English strings of the ko-en pack (cards, dialogue lines, examples, `{{ex}}`, check options, practice o/a/x/q): 0 hits. A first draft hit `tag` (*baggage tag*); *tag* is now matched only inside *tag kis*. *tug*, *no*, *me*, *los*, *tom*, *tub* and *zoo* are left out because they are also English words or names.
   - Tested against the sister course's Hmong: of the 157 lesson examples, 2 sentences of three or more words have no hit (*Pw zoo os.*, *Lem sab xis.*). The misses among cards are single words, and cueTest is applied only to sentences.
   - The validator applies cueTest to mc questions and type hints only. A `nativeTest` field does not exist in lib.js, so "a `why` must be Hmong" is enforced by `check.js` E2 (see requests).
3. **Loanwords** (validate.js: with `nativeScript: null`, `lib.js nativeTester` fails a native string equal to its English counterpart after lower-casing and removing spaces and `. ! ? , … " “ ” ~`; brackets are kept). The convention, in order:
   1. an attested Hmong word;
   2. an attested Hmong head noun + the English loan, as the agencies write it (*mob cancer*, *txiv orange*), e.g. «tsev hotel», «khw McDonald's»;
   3. alphabet «A loj, a me»;
   4. a short Hmong gloss;
   5. replacing the card through a deck deviation.

   `check.js` W3 flags `pizza (pizza)`, which the validator would pass.
4. **Deck count 25,** as asked.
   - The reference has **21** decks, and no ko-based sibling has 25 (vi 23, kn 22); 25 is the es-reference count.
   - The four additions are each motivated by the profile:
     - 22 «Final sounds: -s, -ed» (vi copy, profile §8);
     - 23 «Kuv thiab kuv tsev neeg» (he-she-it, §8);
     - 24 «Lus Hmoob thiab lus Askiv» (§4 error zones, modelled on vi's calque deck);
     - 25 «Nyob hauv Amelika» (§5 learners, §9).
   - Four names keep English where no attested Hmong exists: Phrasal verbs; Pronunciation: …; Movies, TV thiab slang; Final sounds.
   - Each Hmong word was checked with rpa_check and coverage. The only report is the letter A in «A txog Z: lus Askiv».
5. **Lesson split.**
   - B has the 12 lessons of lessons.js. C has the 16 of a1.js, including `final-sounds`.
   - D has the 13 of a2.js and b1.js plus **he-she-it**, written at the end of a2.js. gen.js orders lessons by ORDER plus lessonOrder, not by file. This keeps C at 16.
6. **Localisation map** is fixed by the lead and given item by item in every brief (HMONG RULES §4), so all files agree:
   - Seoul → St. Paul, Busan → Fresno, Daegu → Fresno, Jeju → Thailand, won → dollars, Hanoi (in the vi-copied final-sounds) → St. Paul;
   - Korean → Hmong, "Your English is really good!", Korea → Minnesota / Laos / Thailand by context;
   - kimchi → pho (*fawm*), steak → chicken, Medium rare → Not too spicy, speaks Korean → speaks Chinese;
   - K-pop → music, bias → favorite player, Han Kang → Kao Kalia Yang (added to the local ALLOW.txt);
   - the "suffering" distractors → the kn/ur pair, tiger → cat, subway → bus;
   - Korean embassy → "I need a Hmong interpreter.", Minji → Mai, Mr. Kim → Mr. Vang.

   The 14 deck cards and the shared practice and check items were listed by a scan of the scaffold pack; `check.js` E3 re-runs that scan. Every new target was checked against the ko-en targets for duplicates. Every replacement has an attested Hmong side: *Thaib teb*, *Nplog teb*, *fawm*, *nqaij qaib*, *ntsim*, *daus las*, *lus Suav*, *nkauj*, *neeg txhais lus*, or an ALLOW name. Papaya salad (no Hmong word) is not used.
7. **NEW-WORDS files.**
   - Content: `tools/content/hmn/NEW-WORDS.tsv`. Interface: `tools/i18n/hmn/NEW-WORDS.tsv`.
   - Both have the same columns as `tools/target/hmn/NEW-WORDS.tsv`, plus the source URL. Append with `>>` only.

8. **Interface (role I).**
   - Attested Hmong wherever the glossary or US government, health or school Hmong pages support it. I may open such pages and records each new word with its URL.
