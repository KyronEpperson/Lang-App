# Telugu — compliments plan
**How compliments work here:** Telugu speakers praise food first. Guests praise the meal, and hosts keep serving. Effort, children, a sari or new clothes, and a foreigner's Telugu also get plenty of praise. Everyday praise mixes in English freely ("super", "sweet", "idea"). The usual answer is modest: అయ్యో, అంత లేదండి ("oh, it's not that much") or "I'm still learning", often with the polite -అండి added. A plain "thanks" is fine too. Foreigners' usual mistakes: saying శభాష్ ("bravo") to an elder, which sounds like grading them, and using the intimate నువ్వు ("you") with someone they have just met.
**Who you are talking to:** the polite మీరు ("you") with -ఆరు verbs, as the course does. Telugu verbs do not change with the speaker's gender, so no man/woman forms are needed. Words for children change: పాప (little girl) and బాబు (little boy).

**How forward is this deck:** mild to warm, polite మీరు except cards 15 and 16. Open flirting with a stranger is unusual and can offend, especially in smaller towns. Most of this deck is for people who already know each other or are on a date, and the notes will say so. Left out: film dialogue lines, slang like "figure", and comments on the body.

## Left out, and why
- అదిరింది / అదుర్స్ / కేక ("it rocks"): film and youth slang. Fun, but it dates fast.
- అందగాడు / అందగత్తె ("handsome man" / "beautiful woman"): fine words, but they are said about people more than to them.
- Literary praise words (ప్రశంస, అద్భుతం in speeches): too formal for the spoken course.
- Card 19 is a question ("Where did you learn Telugu?"), not a compliment, but learners hear it right after the compliment.

## Open questions for Kii
1. Flirty cards 15 and 16 switch to the intimate నువ్వు ("you"). Keep them, or keep the whole deck on the polite మీరు?
2. Several cards mix in English (ఐడియా, స్వీట్, ఇంట్రెస్ట్, టైం, as real speakers do). Keep them, or prefer pure Telugu where it exists?

## Needs a native check first
- #13 నప్పింది ("suits you"): is it common in both Andhra and Telangana?
- The replies #19 అయ్యో, అంత లేదండి, #21 మీ అభిమానం and #22 ఏదో, మామూలుగా చేశాను.
- Flirty #4 ఏది వేసుకున్నా బాగుంటారు, #7 టైం తెలియదు and #23 ఆల్రెడీ ఒకరు ఉన్నారు.
