# Thai — compliments plan
**How compliments work here:** Thais compliment often and lightly, on food, skill, looks, children and anyone who tries a few words of Thai ("your Thai is so good!" follows a single สวัสดี). Comments on looks are freer than in English, including remarks on weight or skin, which Thais may mean kindly but a learner should never copy. Praise is answered modestly: "oh no, not really", "you're flattering me", often with a smile and no thank-you. Accepting with a plain "thank you" is fine for a foreigner.
**Who you are talking to:** the cards are written in plain form and the learner adds ครับ (khráp, men) or ค่ะ (khâ, women), as the course does where one card is enough. "You" is คุณ or simply left out, which is very natural in Thai. Where a card has "I", it shows the woman's ฉัน and the note gives the man's ผม.

**How forward is this deck:** mostly mild and warm; three bold cards (14–16). Thais flirt playfully and indirectly, so the teasing replies (17, 18) matter as much as the compliments. Left out on purpose: comments on skin, weight or body, cheesy "joke" lines, and anything to service staff, where it would be inappropriate.

## Left out, and why
- อ้วนขึ้นนะ / ผอมลงนะ ("you've gained / lost weight"): common small talk, but a foreigner should not copy it.
- ขาว ("fair-skinned") as praise: common, but it carries colorism a learner should not repeat.
- Pet names (ที่รัก "darling", ตัวเอง): for couples only, too intimate here.
- จีบ ("to court someone") slang lines and มุก (cheesy pick-up lines): they only work as jokes.
- หล่อมาก ("very handsome") as a separate Deck 1 card: covered by Deck 2 #2 and Deck 1 #13.

2. Flirty deck: use คุณ (polite "you", my plan) or the softer, more intimate เธอ / เรา that young couples use?
