# Brazilian Portuguese — compliments plan
**How compliments work here:** Brazilians compliment often and warmly: food, the home, children ("Que gracinha!"), how someone looks, and effort. A compliment is often brushed off modestly ("Que nada!" = "Oh, not at all!") or answered with "Bondade sua" ("That's kind of you"); a flat "thank you" is fine but can sound a bit cool. Your Portuguese will be praised generously. Speaker gender matters in replies: a man says *obrigado*, a woman *obrigada*.
**Who you are talking to:** *você*, the everyday "you" of the course; *o senhor / a senhora* for older people goes in the notes. Words that change for a woman (engraçado → engraçada) show both.

**How forward is this deck:** first-date level, with one bolder card (*Estou a fim de você*). Left out on purpose: body comments, *cantadas* (cheesy pick-up lines) and slang like *gata / gato* (= hot).

## Left out, and why
- *Te amo*: "I love you", too strong.
- *Gata / gato, gostosa / gostoso*: slang about looks; *gostosa* is sexual.
- *Cantadas* (pick-up lines): jokey, often cheesy or crude.
- *Imagina!*: mainly a reply to thanks, not to a compliment, so it would confuse.
- European Portuguese forms: the course is Brazilian.
