# Kannada — compliments plan
**How compliments work here:** Kannada speakers praise food first. Guests praise the meal ("the food was super!"), and hosts keep serving. Effort, children, a sari or new clothes, and a foreigner's Kannada also get plenty of praise. Bengaluru speech mixes in English freely ("super", "idea", "sweet"). The usual answer is modest: ಅಯ್ಯೋ, ಹಾಗೇನಿಲ್ಲ ("oh, not really") or "I'm still learning". A plain "thanks" is fine too. Foreigners' usual mistakes: saying ಭೇಷ್ ("bravo") to an elder, which sounds like grading them, and using the intimate ನೀನು ("you") with someone they have just met.
**Who you are talking to:** the polite ನೀವು ("you") with -ಈರಿ verbs, as the course does, in the course's spoken Bengaluru spellings (ಮಾಡ್ತೀರಿ, ಮಾಡಿದ್ರಿ). Kannada verbs do not change with the speaker's gender for "I" and "you", so no man/woman forms are needed.

**How forward is this deck:** mild to warm, polite ನೀವು except cards 15 and 16. Open flirting with a stranger is unusual and can offend outside young city circles. Most of this deck is for people who already know each other or are on a date, and the notes will say so. Left out: film dialogue lines, slang, and comments on the body.

## Left out, and why
- ಸಕ್ಕತ್ತಾಗಿದೆ / ಮಸ್ತ್ ("awesome"): Bengaluru slang. Common, but too casual for a deck in the polite form.
- ಚೆಲುವೆ / ಚೆಲುವ ("beauty", "handsome one"): poetic and from songs; it sounds odd when a learner says it.
- ಸೂಪರ್ alone: everyone says it, but it is one English word, not a phrase to learn.
- Card 19 is a question ("Where did you learn Kannada?"), not a compliment, but learners hear it right after the compliment.

## Open questions for Kii
1. Flirty cards 15 and 16 switch to the intimate ನೀನು ("you"). Keep them, or keep the whole deck on the polite ನೀವು?
2. Several cards mix in English (ಸೂಪರ್, ಐಡಿಯಾ, ಸ್ವೀಟ್, ಇಂಟರೆಸ್ಟ್, ಟೈಮ್, as Bengaluru speakers do). Keep them, or prefer pure Kannada where it exists?

## Needs a native check first
- #3 ಕೈರುಚಿ and #23 ಏನೋ ಸುಮ್ನೆ ಮಾಡಿದೆ: common, and natural as written?
- #19 the spoken polite past ಕಲಿತ್ರಿ, and #22 ಥ್ಯಾಂಕ್ಸ್, ನಿಮ್ಮ ಪ್ರೀತಿ as a reply.
- Flirty #10 ನೋಡಬೇಕು ಅನ್ಸುತ್ತೆ and #14 ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ ನಿಮ್ಮದೇ ಯೋಚನೆ.
- The spoken spellings that are not in the course's spelling table: ಹಾಕ್ಕೊಂಡ್ರೂ, ಅಂದ್ಕೊಂಡಿದ್ದೆ, ಇದ್ರೆ, ಅನ್ಸುತ್ತೆ.
