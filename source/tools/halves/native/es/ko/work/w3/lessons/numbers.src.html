Korean has two sets of numbers and they are not interchangeable.

Used forage, counting things, hoursmoney, dates, minutes, phone numbers, floors

Native numbers 1 to 4 shorten before a counter (the word in parentheses). Sino numbers build like English digits: 십일 = 11, 이십 = 20, 이십오 = 25, 백 = 100.

Korean groups large numbers by 10,000 (만), not 1,000. 50,000 won is 오만 원 (five ten-thousands), not fifty thousands.
Common mistakes
  • 다섯 시 삼십 분 ✓ but 삼 시 ✗ — hours are native (세 시).
  • 하나 개 ✗ — 1–4 shorten before a counter: 한 개.
  • 오십천 원 ✗ — Korean groups by 만: 오만 원.
