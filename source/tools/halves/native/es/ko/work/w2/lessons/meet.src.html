이에요 vs 예요

"I am ___" uses 이에요 after a consonant and 예요 after a vowel. Say the name out loud: if it ends in a vowel sound, use 예요.

Ends inUseExample
consonant이에요학생이에요 · Kim이에요
vowel예요저예요 · Lisa예요

Age is a normal question

Koreans ask 몇 살이에요? early because age sets the politeness level between you. Answer with a native number + 살.
