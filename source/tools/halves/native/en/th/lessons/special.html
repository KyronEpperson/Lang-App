You can now read most Thai syllables with the rules. A handful of spellings break them, and they are common, so this lesson collects them. Then it puts everything together: reading a line with no spaces between words.

Silent letters under ์

The small mark ์ over a letter means "don't say this letter". It is frequent in words borrowed from English and from old Indian languages: เบียร์ biia (beer) ends on the vowel, and ไมค์ mái (the name Mike) has no k. Words borrowed from English also often take a high tone that the spelling does not show, as ไมค์ does; the romanization tells you.

ทร said s, and a silent ร

In a few words ทร is said s: ทราบ sâap, a formal "to know". In จริง jing (true, real) the ร is simply not said.

{{ex:th.s0188}}{{ex:th.s0189}}

Vowel length the spelling hides

Some very common words are spelled with a short vowel but said with a long one: น้ำ náam (water), ได้ dâai (can, to get), เก้า gâao (nine). Others look long but are said short: เล่น lên (to play), ต้อง dtâwng (must), เงิน ngoen (money). There is no rule to spot them; the romanization always shows the length, so trust it.

{{ex:th.s0190}}{{ex:th.s0191}}

The repeat mark ๆ and the short mark ฯ

ๆ repeats the word before it. It often softens or stresses the word, or shows more than one: เด็กๆ is "the kids", ช้าๆ is "slowly". The mark ฯ shortens a long name; you will meet it in กรุงเทพฯ grung-thêep, Bangkok, read without it.

Reading a line with no spaces

Thai writes the words of a phrase together and puts a space only between phrases, where English would put a comma or a full stop. Don't sound out letter by letter. Look for words you know first, and the gaps between them fall into place. On a menu, each line is the dish, then the price.

{{ex:th.s0194}}{{ex:th.s0195}}

In ข้าวผัดกุ้ง you can spot ข้าว (rice), then ผัด (fried), then กุ้ง (shrimp): the main word comes first. Prices are written with ordinary digits and read in Thai.

Most common mistake: sounding out every letter. เบียร์ read with an r at the end → เบียร์ (biia), because ์ silences the ร.
