Lesson 4 covered syllables that end in a vowel or a nasal. This lesson adds the short checked syllables, the light syllable after --, and a word about 仔.

Checked syllables in -p, -t, -k

Checked syllables have only two tones, 4 and 8. When they change in sandhi, they swap: high becomes low and low becomes high (Wikipedia, Tone sandhi; Bite-size Taiwanese, "4 and 8 swap"). So tone 4 becomes tone 8, and tone 8 becomes tone 4. 失禮 sit-lé (I apologize): sit is tone 4, said in tone 8 before lé.

Checked syllables in -h

A syllable ending in -h loses its catch in sandhi: the -h disappears and the syllable takes a full tone. Tone 4 becomes tone 2 (Bite-size Taiwanese; taioaan.org: -h syllables are "usually pronounced in shouting tone", its name for tone 2). Tone 8 becomes tone 3: only Bite-size Taiwanese gives this pair; Wikipedia says such syllables "drop their final consonant to become tone 2 or 3". 肉圓 bah-uân (meat dumpling): bah is said without its catch.

{{ex:nan.s0034}}

As always, the spelling keeps the citation tone: you still write sit, tha̍k, bah.

The neutral tone after --

Two hyphens mark a neutral tone. The syllable after -- is said light and short, without its own tone, and the syllable before it keeps its citation tone instead of changing (the MOE romanization manual: the syllable before -- "唸本調", keeps its own tone). After tones 1, 5 and 7 the light syllable continues the pitch of the one before it. Tâi-lô still prints the light syllable's own mark.

{{ex:nan.s0035}}{{ex:nan.s0036}}{{ex:nan.s0037}}

When you read a line with --, give the syllable before it its full written tone, then say the light one short and soft. In 多謝你, siā keeps tone 7 and lí is said light. Particles at the end of a question, like 未 in 你食飽未？, are light too.

仔

Many nouns end in 仔 á: 桌仔 toh-á (table). The syllable before 仔 changes in its own way, which this course does not cover. Copy the word and its Tâi-lô as you meet it.

Most common mistake: giving the light syllable a full tone. 多謝你 with a full, falling lí → To-siā--lí, with lí light and short.
