In the fall and winter, Hmong communities in Fresno, Sacramento, St. Paul, Milwaukee and many other places hold New Year festivals. This lesson gives you the words you will see and hear there.

Noj peb caug

The celebration is noj peb caug falling · high · falling-breathy. Word by word it is "eat thirty": noj is eat and peb caug is thirty. The New Year itself is xyoo tshiab mid · high, "new year", and the greeting is Nyob zoo xyoo tshiab!, "Happy New Year!".

The ball toss

Young people stand in two lines and throw a ball back and forth: pov pob rising · high. It is a perfect tone drill: pov (rising tone) is "throw" and pob (high tone) is "ball".

{{ex:hmn.s0200}}

Music and needlework

The qeej falling is a bamboo reed pipe, played at New Year and at weddings. paj ntaub falling · high is Hmong needlework: embroidered cloth and story cloths. Keep both words in English too; there is no good translation.

New clothes

People dress up: hnav rising is "to wear", khaub ncaws high · low is "clothes", and tshiab is "new". Many wear a hlab nyiaj high · falling, a sash hung with silver coins.

{{ex:hmn.s0203}}{{ex:hmn.s0204}}

As always, the describing word comes after the noun: "new clothes" is khaub ncaws tshiab, clothes first.
