Plain partner, ejective partner

You already know the plain partners. ተ is a plain t. ጠ is not that t. ቸ is a plain ch. ጨ is not that ch. ፐ is a plain p. ጰ is not that p. ሰ is a plain s. ጸ is not that s. Each ejective has a catch in the throat. Listen to the pair. Do not flatten the ejective into the plain letter. Tap each pair: the plain letter on the top line, its ejective partner under it. ቀ, under ከ, is the pair you met first.

ፀ is the same sound as ጸ. Two letters, one ejective ts, the way ሀ and ሐ are one h. Keep the spelling the word actually uses. Do not pick one letter and use it everywhere.

Words you can read now

ደ is d, so ደህና is no longer only a shape. It is ደ, sixth-order ሀ, then ና. ጤና is health: fifth-order ጠ, the ejective, then ና. ቡና is coffee. ገና is the word from the first lesson. You can read the letters now. You still cannot see whether the n is long.

You now know every basic row. The cards below are everyday words: food, family, numbers, directions. Read each one character at a time. Decide for each sixth order whether it keeps its vowel, then flip the card and check the long consonants.
