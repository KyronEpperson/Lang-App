English can point at the digit 2 and be finished. Amharic has a word for each number, written in the fidel. The digit is not the word. Learn the word and say that.

NumberWordNumberWord
1አንድ7ሰባት
2ሁለት8ስምንት
3ሶስት9ዘጠኝ
4አራት10አስር
5አምስት20ሃያ
6ስድስት100መቶ

You will also see three written ሦስት, ten written ዐስር, and twenty written ሀያ. Same words. This course writes ሶስት, አስር and ሃያ. Eleven through nineteen set አስራ in front of the small number: አስራ አንድ is eleven. The other tens are their own words. Leave them until a price needs them. Twenty is not "two tens."

After a number, the line to learn is singular

English adds an s: two houses. The line to learn is the singular noun: ሁለት ቤት. A plural after a number also occurs, including ሁለት ድመቶች. Do not treat that plural as a mistake. Do not add an English s. ቤትስ is not a word. The copula still notices how many there are. One masculine thing takes ነው. More than one takes ናቸው, the verb ending for they. When someone asks how many, answer with the number word and ናቸው. Do not read the fidel as if it were the digit. Say the word out loud: one through ten, then twenty, then a hundred.

A number does not freeze the person

The verb ending still matches who you mean. A woman takes the feminine ናት. A man takes ነው. To a man, ነህ. To a woman, ነሽ. To a stranger or an older person, the polite form ነዎት. እኛ takes ነን. Counting people uses the same split.
