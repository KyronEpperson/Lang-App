A server in Addis Ababa is a stranger unless you know them, so please is እባክዎ. If you are talking to a woman you know, please is እባክሽ. እባክህ is only for a man. The food still comes first, then እፈልጋለሁ, the want you already use. Please does not change that ending. You are the one who wants the food, so the verb stays the I form.

A simple meal has three beats. You sit, you name one dish and one drink, and you ask for the bill. ውሃ is water. አምቦ ውሃ is the bottled water on the menu. The spicy stew is ቀይ ወጥ. The mild stew is አልጫ ወጥ. Order them one at a time, each with እፈልጋለሁ at the end.

What the manual's menu prints

These are the dishes and drinks to learn. እንጀራ is the flat bread the meal is built on. ፍርፍር is that bread torn into a sauce, which is why the manual treats እንጀራ as its base. ክትፎ is finely chopped beef, often served raw. ጥብስ is fried meat. እንቁላል ጥብስ is eggs cooked with pepper and onion, not plain fried eggs. You do not need every row on the first visit. Learn injera, one stew, water, coffee, and the bill, then add the rest from the table. The book's prices are practice figures, not today's menu. You already saw coffee at four birr there.

The bill, and sit

Ask for the bill the way you ask a price: ሂሳብ ስንት ነው. One bill, so ነው. If someone tells you to sit, the command matches you, the person being told. Said to a woman, it is ተቀመጪ, with እባክሽ. Said to a stranger, it is እባክዎ ይቀመጡ.
