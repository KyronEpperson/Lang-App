English adds the word not, and leaves the verb sitting there: I did not go, I do not go. Amharic does not have a separate not. It wraps the verb. አል goes on the front and ም goes on the end. You already met one pair, አለኝ and የለኝም. አለ keeps that shape. Do not wrap it as አላለኝም. Go and drink take አል and ም.

The past wrap

Wrap the past form you know. ሄድኩ becomes አልሄድኩም, I did not go. The person and the gender stay inside the wrap.

do not goአልሄድምአትሄድምአትሄጂምአይሄድምአትሄድምአይሄዱም

The woman form of the past is አልሄድሽም. The man form is አልሄድክም. She is አልሄደችም. He is አልሄደም. That is not አልሄድም, which is present. Polite, and they, share አልሄዱም. Drink wraps the same way: ቡናውን አልጠጣሁም, I did not drink the coffee. The object ending stays.

The present is a short form

Do not wrap the long present. አልእሄዳለሁም is not a word. Both "I do not go" and "I will not go" are አልሄድም. For he, the form is አይሄድም. You, a woman, are አትሄጂም: the consonant changes, so listen. You, a man, and she, share the letters አትሄድም. Say አንተ or እሷ when you need to show which. The polite form is አይሄዱም. For እኛ, it is አንሄድም.

ነው has its own negative, not this wrap. I am not is አይደለሁም. You, a man, are not is አይደለህም. You, a woman, are not is አይደለሽም. He or it is not is አይደለም. She is not is አይደለችም. The polite form is አይደሉም.

Do not keep the long present ending inside the negative. አልእሄዳለሁም → አልሄድም.
