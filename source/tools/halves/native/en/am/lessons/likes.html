Like works the same way as want. The thing comes first, the verb last. "I like coffee" is ቡና እወዳለሁ. "I don't like coffee" is ቡና አልወድም. በጣም means very, and it sits in front of the verb: ቡና በጣም እወዳለሁ, I like coffee a lot. Want and like are not the same verb. እፈልጋለሁ means you want it. እወዳለሁ means you like it. You can want a coffee and also like coffee, but you cannot swap the words.

One ending is not enough

The verb ending matches the person and the gender. ትወዳለህ is the man form. ትወጃለሽ is the woman form. He is ይወዳል. She is ትወዳለች. ይወዳሉ is the polite form. The negative man form is አትወድም. That same spelling is also "she doesn't like". The words around it tell you which. The woman form is different: አትወጅም.

Kind remarks

The manual gives ውብ for a man or a masculine thing, and ውቢት as the feminine word. ቆንጆ stays ቆንጆ in the manual's sentence about a woman. It does not grow a feminine ending. The copula still changes: ናት for her, ነው for him. ውብ ነህ talks to a man about him. ውቢት ነሽ talks to a woman about her. ቆንጆ ናት talks about a woman. Once coffee is the cup you both mean, it is ቡናው, and the manual says ቡናው በጣም ቆንጆ ነው.

መልካም ቀን is a wish for a good day. መልካም ጉዞ wishes someone a good trip. በጣም አሪፍ ነው means it's great. These are things you say to someone. They are not like-sentences, and they do not need እወዳለሁ. A set reply to a compliment is not in the sources this course uses, so say the remark and stop. This lesson does not teach a reply line.
