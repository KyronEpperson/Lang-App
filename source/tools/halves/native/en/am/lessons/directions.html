Where is the hotel? is ሆቴሉ የት ነው. The hotel is the subject, so the verb ending is ነው, last. የት is where, which you already know. To a stranger, start with ይቅርታ or with ጤና ይስጥልኝ.

{{ex:am.s0221}}{{ex:am.s0222}}

Right, left, straight

ወደ ቀኝ is to the right. ወደ ግራ is to the left. ቀጥ ብለው ይሂዱ is go straight, said politely. The place comes before the verb. On the street, stop a stranger with ይቅርታ, ask where the hotel is, and listen for a place and then ነው. If they tell you to walk, the command matches you. ሂድ is the man form, ሂጂ is the woman form, and ይሂዱ is the polite form. Go right, said to a woman, is ወደ ቀኝ ሂጂ.

{{ex:am.s0223}}{{ex:am.s0224}}{{ex:am.s0225}}{{ex:am.s0226}}

Where are you?

When the subject is "you", ነው is the wrong ending. የት ነህ is the man form. የት ነሽ is the woman form. የት ነዎት is the polite form. የት ነው asks where a thing is, or where he is. It does not ask where you are. Do not answer a hotel question with ነህ, and do not ask a woman የት ነው when you mean where she is. A woman's name takes ናት, as with family: the person, not the street, decides the ending.

{{ex:am.s0227}}{{ex:am.s0228}}{{ex:am.s0229}}

በአዲስ አበባ is in Addis Ababa. በ- is not a separate word sitting where English puts "in". It attaches to the next word. ወደ stays a word of its own in ወደ ቀኝ and ወደ ግራ. The place still comes before ነው. After you hear the direction, አመሰግናለሁ is the thank-you you already know. The hotel question and "where are you?" use the same where-word, የት. Only the ending changes, and it changes for the subject. A polite "where are you?" is የት ነዎት. A polite "where is the hotel?" is still ሆቴሉ የት ነው, because the hotel is not እርስዎ.

{{ex:am.s0230}}
The verb before the place is the slip: ነው ወደ ቀኝ → ወደ ቀኝ ነው. And "where are you?" to a man is not the hotel's verb: የት ነው → የት ነህ.
