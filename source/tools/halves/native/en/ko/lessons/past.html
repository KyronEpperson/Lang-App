You already know the hard part. Take the present 요-form, drop 요, and add ㅆ어요: 가요 → 갔어요, 먹어요 → 먹었어요, 해요 → 했어요.

Describing verbs and 있어요 work the same way

"Was good", "was there" and "had" are just the past of the word you know.

"Was a …": 이었어요 / 였어요

After a consonant use 이었어요, after a vowel 였어요.

Time words go first

어제 (yesterday), 아까 (a little while ago) and 지난주 (last week) set the scene at the start.

{{ex:ko.s0095}}{{ex:ko.s0096}}{{ex:ko.s0097}}
Korean has one past tense. "I ate", "I have eaten" and "I did eat" are all 먹었어요.
Common mistakes
  • 가었어요 ✗ → 갔어요 ✓ (start from 가요, not 가다)
  • 하었어요 ✗ → 했어요 ✓
  • 학생였어요 ✗ → 학생이었어요 ✓ (consonant → 이었어요)
  • 갔어요 안 ✗ → 안 갔어요 ✓
