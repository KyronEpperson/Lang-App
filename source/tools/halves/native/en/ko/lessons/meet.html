이에요 vs 예요

"I am ___" uses 이에요 after a final consonant and 예요 after a vowel. A name follows its Hangul spelling, not how it sounds in your language, so look at the last Hangul block: Mike = 마이크 → 마이크예요, Juan = 후안 → 후안이에요.

Age is a normal question

Koreans ask 몇 살이에요? early because age sets the politeness level between you. Answer with a native number + 살.
