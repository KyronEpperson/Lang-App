Politeness (요) is about the listener. Honorifics (시) are about the subject — the person doing the action. Use them for elders, customers, teachers, and your partner's parents.

The rule

Insert -(으)시- after the stem: 가다 → 가시다 → 가세요 (present) / 가셨어요 (past). 으시 after a consonant: 앉다 → 앉으세요.
