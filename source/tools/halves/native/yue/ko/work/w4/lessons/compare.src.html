Korean describing verbs never change shape to compare. You just add small words: 보다 (than), 더 (more), 덜 (less) and 제일 (the most).

보다 = than

Attach 보다 to the thing you are comparing against. 더 is optional but common.

Order is flexible

Because 보다 marks the "than" item, the two nouns can swap places.

더 = more, 덜 = less

They go right before the describing word, or stand alone with a verb.

보다 here is a particle stuck to a noun. It only looks like the verb 보다 (to see); the verb is conjugated (봐요), the particle never changes.
Common mistakes
  • 보다 on the wrong noun — 버스보다 택시가 싸요 means the taxi is cheaper
  • 더 제일 맛있어요 ✗ → 제일 맛있어요 ✓ (pick one)
  • 택시 보다 ✗ → 택시보다 ✓ (no space; it's a particle)
  • 더 after the adjective — 싸요 더 ✗ → 더 싸요 ✓
