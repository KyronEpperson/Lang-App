Three endings cover every invitation. Attach them to the verb stem.

EndingFeelExample
-(으)ㄹ까요?"Shall we…?" — soft suggestion커피 마실까요? / 카페 갈까?
-(으)ㄹ래요?"Do you want to…?" — asks their wish같이 갈래요? / 영화 볼래?
-자"Let's…" — casual, decided놀자! / 밥 먹자

ㄹ after a vowel stem (가 → 갈까요), 을 after a consonant stem (먹 → 먹을까요).

Saying yes and no

{{ex:ko.s0202}}{{ex:ko.s0203}}{{ex:ko.s0204}}
Koreans confirm meetups with place + exit number, not addresses: 강남역 11번 출구. Learn your station's exits.
Common mistakes
  • 볼래요 to an elder — use 보실래요 or 볼까요?
  • Giving an address — Koreans say station + exit: 강남역 11번 출구.
  • Saying yes with 네 only — add 좋아요 or 콜 so it sounds enthusiastic.
