For plans and predictions, add ㄹ 거예요 to a stem that ends in a vowel and 을 거예요 to one that ends in a consonant. It means "will" or "going to".

Vowel stem + ㄹ 거예요

The ㄹ tucks under the last syllable.

{{ex:ko.s0098}}{{ex:ko.s0099}}{{ex:ko.s0100}}

Consonant stem + 을 거예요

Stems that already end in ㄹ, like 살다 (live), just add 거예요: 살 거예요.

{{ex:ko.s0101}}{{ex:ko.s0102}}

Plans, with a time word

내일 (tomorrow), 이따가 (later today) and 주말에 (on the weekend) go at the start.

{{ex:ko.s0103}}{{ex:ko.s0104}}{{ex:ko.s0105}}{{ex:ko.s0106}}

Guessing

With describing verbs it means "probably".

{{ex:ko.s0107}}{{ex:ko.s0108}}
거예요 is pronounced with a tight k, like "kkeoyeyo". For something fixed and soon, the plain present works too: 내일 가요 (I'm going tomorrow).
Common mistakes
  • 가을 거예요 ✗ → 갈 거예요 ✓ (vowel stem → ㄹ)
  • 먹ㄹ 거예요 ✗ → 먹을 거예요 ✓ (consonant stem → 을)
  • 갈 거 안 예요 ✗ → 안 갈 거예요 ✓
  • 갈거에요 ✗ → 갈 거예요 ✓ (it is spelled 예요)
