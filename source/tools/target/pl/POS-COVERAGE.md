Writer EN-PL-CORE, 2026-11-23. Read from `lessons-1.js`, `lessons-2.js` and `lessons-3.js`: every lesson's title and section headings, plus word searches across the three files and the lesson plan in `docs/profiles/en-pl.md` §8. No lesson was written or changed.

**Key**
- **taught**: a lesson presents it as grammar, with forms and practice.
- **touched**: it appears inside another lesson's topic, or only part of the class is taught.
- **missing**: no lesson presents it.

## Summary

- **Missing:** conjunctions; interjections.
- **Touched only:** adverbs as a class; pronouns beyond the subject set and *mój* (object, prepositional, indefinite and negative forms); determiners (*ten, każdy, żaden*); ordinals outside telling the time.
- **Taught:** nouns with four cases; verbs (present classes, imperfective past and future, motion, three "know" verbs); adjective agreement; the case-driving prepositions; cardinal numbers with złoty.

## Proposed lessons

Each outline links to a new core deck. The place suggested is after the lessons each one builds on.

### 1. `conjunctions`: i, a, ale, że, bo: joining two ideas (after `questions`)
- **Teaches:** *i* (and) vs *a* (and, with a contrast or a new subject); *ale*; *albo*; *że* after *wiem, myślę, mówię*, always after a comma; *bo* (casual) and *ponieważ*; *jeśli* and *kiedy* with the present.
- **English-speaker trap:** using *i* for every "and" (*Ja piję kawę i ty herbatę* instead of *a ty herbatę*); dropping *że* the way English drops "that" (*Myślę to dobry pomysł*); leaving out the comma before *że, bo, kiedy*.
- **Examples:** *Ja piję kawę, a Anna herbatę.* · *Wiem, że jesteś zmęczony.* · *Jeśli masz czas, zadzwoń.*
- **Deck:** Top conjunctions (18 cards already have examples).

### 2. `interjections`: au, ojej, łał, halo: reacting in Polish (after `greetings`)
- **Teaches:** 12–15 reactions grouped by feeling: pain (*au, ała*), surprise and delight (*och, ach, łał, jejku, rety*), sympathy and dismay (*ojej, oj, szkoda*), relief (*uff*), disgust (*fuj*), praise (*brawo, super*), and the social ones (*hej, pa, dzięki, halo*). *Kurczę* as the polite stand-in for a swear word.
- **English-speaker trap:** carrying over *ouch, oops, wow* unchanged (Polish has *au, ups, łał*, and *łał* is spelled with *ł*); using *hej, pa, dzięki* with a stranger, who gets *Dzień dobry, Do widzenia, Dziękuję*; the swear words in film subtitles, which are much stronger than they look.
- **Examples:** *Au! Moja ręka!* · *Ojej, nie mam portfela!* · *Halo? Dzień dobry, tu Anna.*
- **Deck:** Top interjections (every card has a dialogue).

### 3. `pronouns`: mnie, cię, go, ją: pronouns as objects (after `accusative`)
- **Teaches:** the accusative pronouns (*mnie, cię / ciebie, go / jego, ją, nas, was, ich*); short forms after a verb, long forms for stress and after a preposition (*dla ciebie*), *n-* after a preposition (*dla niego, z nią*); the dative trio met in set phrases (*mi, mu, jej*: *Podoba mi się*).
- **English-speaker trap:** the subject form as an object (*Lubię ty* for *Lubię cię*); the short form after a preposition (*dla cię*); *go* for a woman.
- **Examples:** *Znam go.* · *Kocham cię.* · *To jest dla ciebie.*
- **Deck:** Top pronouns.

### 4. `adverbs`: szybko, ładnie, już, jeszcze (after `routine`)
- **Teaches:** adverbs from adjectives with *-o* and *-e* / *-ie* (*szybki → szybko, ładny → ładnie, dobry → dobrze*); *już / jeszcze / jeszcze nie*; the double negative with *nigdy, nigdzie, nic, nikt* (*Nikt nie wie*); *dużo, mało, trochę* + genitive.
- **English-speaker trap:** an agreeing adjective where Polish needs the adverb (*Mówisz dobry*); one negative only (*Nigdy piję*); the base form after *dużo* (*dużo ludzie* for *dużo ludzi*).
- **Examples:** *Proszę mówić wolno.* · *Jeszcze nie wiem.* · *Nic nie rozumiem.*
- **Deck:** Top adverbs (with *nic, nikt* from Top pronouns).

### Smaller gaps (a section in an existing lesson, not a new lesson)
- **ten / ta / to / te and tamten:** one section in `gender` or `adjectives`; links to Top pronouns.
- **Ordinals and *dwie*:** one section in `numbers` (*dwie kawy*; *pierwszy, drugi, trzeci* for floors and dates); links to Top words.
- **Comparatives** (*lepszy, większy, niż*): one section in `adjectives`; links to Top adjectives and *niż* in Top conjunctions.
