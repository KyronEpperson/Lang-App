## Proposed lessons

### 1. Linking ideas: 抑是, 毋過, 若是, 因為, 所以
- **Teaches:** joining two sentences with or, but, if … then (若是 … 就), because, so, although … but (雖然 … 毋過), after (… 了後).
- **English-speaker trap:** using 佮 for "and" between sentences (佮 only joins nouns). Also dropping 就 after 若是, and putting the clause word at the end as in English ("I'll go if…").
- **Examples:** 你欲啉茶抑是汽水？ / 若是落雨，我就無欲去。 / 因為落雨，我無去。
- **Links to:** `Top conjunctions`.

### 2. Reacting: 有影無？, 毋著, 小心！, 無法度
- **Teaches:** short reactions and replies in conversation: surprise, disagreeing, warning, giving in (好啦), accepting that nothing can be done.
- **English-speaker trap:** answering a yes/no question with 是 for "yes" every time. Taigi repeats the verb (有, 會, 欲) or uses 著 / 毋著. The particle 啦 is also mistaken for rudeness when it only softens.
- **Examples:** 伊是老師。— 有影無？ / 小心！車來矣。 / 無法度，咱坐計程車。
- **Links to:** `Top interjections`, `Top particles`.

### 3. To, from, with, until: the coverbs 共, 對, 用, 到
- **Teaches:** coverbs before the verb: 共 (to, for, and marking the object), 對 (from, toward), 用 (with, using), 到 (until, to); place words after the noun (邊仔, 對面, 中央).
- **English-speaker trap:** putting the coverb phrase after the verb, as English does ("I come from Tainan" → *我來對臺南; right: 我對臺南來).
- **Examples:** 伊對臺南來。 / 我共你講。 / 我蹛佇公園邊仔。
- **Links to:** `Top prepositions`.

### 4. All, again, already, often: adverbs before the verb
- **Teaches:** 攏 (all; it comes after the people it covers), 閣 / 再 (again: now or next time), 已經 … 矣, 定定, 有時, 做陣.
- **English-speaker trap:** putting "all" before the noun (*攏𪜶 for "all of them"; right: 𪜶攏…), and using 閣 for a "next time" again where 再 fits.
- **Examples:** 𪜶攏是學生。 / 伊已經來矣。 / 阮定定去公園運動。
- **Links to:** `Top adverbs`.

### 5. Mine and yours: 的, 家己, 逐家
- **Teaches:** pronoun + 的 for possession (我的冊, 這是我的), dropping 的 with family and home (阮阿母, 恁厝), and 家己, 逐家, 別人.
- **English-speaker trap:** adding 的 everywhere (我的阿母 is possible, but 阮阿母 is the normal way), and using 我 for "my" with family where Taigi uses the plural 阮.
- **Examples:** 這本冊是我的。 / 我家己去就好。 / 逐家攏來矣。
- **Links to:** `Top pronouns`.
