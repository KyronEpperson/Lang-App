# Parts of speech in the en-pt lessons

Writer EN-PT-CORE, 2026-11-23. Read from `lessons-1.js`, `lessons-2.js` and `lessons-3.js`: every lesson's section headings, the full bodies of the lessons that matter for each class, and word searches across all three files. No lesson was written or changed.

**Key**
- **taught**: a lesson presents it as grammar, with forms and practice.
- **touched**: it appears inside another lesson's topic, or only part of the class is taught.
- **missing**: no lesson presents it.

## Summary

- **Missing:** interjections; indefinite and negative pronouns (*alguém, ninguém, nada, tudo*); ordinal numbers.
- **Touched only:** adverbs as a class (no *sempre / nunca*, no *ainda*, no *-mente*, no double negative); subordinating conjunctions (*que, se, enquanto, embora*); comparisons.
- **Taught:** nouns, verbs (A1 scope), adjectives, personal, possessive, demonstrative and question pronouns, prepositions with contractions, articles, cardinal numbers.

## Proposed lessons

Outlines only. Each one links to a new core deck and is placed after the lessons it builds on.

### 1. `interjections`: Nossa!, Ai!, Ué!: reacting in Portuguese (after `connectors`)
- **Teaches:** the 12–15 exclamations Brazilians really use, grouped by feeling: surprise (*Nossa!, Caramba!, Eita!, Caraca!*), pain and worry (*Ai!, Ai, meu Deus!*), confusion (*Ué!*), relief (*Ufa!, Graças a Deus!*), hope (*Tomara!*), sympathy (*Coitado! / Coitada!*, which agrees), and the replies *Imagina!* and *Que isso!*. Also the pattern *Que + adjective!* with agreement (*Que linda!*).
- **English-speaker trap:** carrying over *wow / oops / ouch*; reading *Nossa!* as "our"; *Coitado!* not agreeing with a woman; saying *de nada* where Brazilians say *Imagina!*.
- **Examples:** *Nossa! Que caro!* · *Ué, o banco está fechado?* · *Coitada! Ela está doente.*
- **Deck:** Top interjections (every card already has a dialogue).

### 2. `adverbs`: sempre, nunca, ainda, já, and -mente (after `past`)
- **Teaches:** frequency words and where they go (*sempre, às vezes, quase nunca, nunca*, before the verb); *já / ainda / ainda não*; adverbs in *-mente* on the feminine form (*rápida → rapidamente*, *normal → normalmente*); the double negative (*Não… nunca / nada / ninguém*).
- **English-speaker trap:** *rapidomente* built on the masculine; dropping *não* in *Não vejo ninguém*; *ainda* translated as "even"; putting *sempre* at the end of the sentence.
- **Examples:** *Eu nunca como carne. / Não como carne nunca.* · *Você já almoçou? Ainda não.* · *Normalmente eu acordo cedo.*
- **Deck:** Top adverbs (with *nada, ninguém* from Top pronouns).

### 3. `que-se`: Joining ideas: que, se, enquanto, por isso, do que (after `connectors`)
- **Teaches:** *que* after verbs of thinking and saying (*Acho que…, Ele disse que…*); *se* + present for real conditions (*Se você quer, eu vou*); *enquanto*; *por isso* and *então* for results; comparisons with *mais / menos … do que*, *melhor, pior, maior, menor*. Present and past only; the subjunctive after *embora, caso, para que* is for recognition.
- **English-speaker trap:** dropping *que* the way English drops "that" (*Acho vai chover*); *mais bom* instead of *melhor*; *se* (if) confused with the pronoun *se*.
- **Examples:** *Acho que vai chover.* · *Se você tem tempo, vamos almoçar.* · *Ele é mais alto do que eu.*
- **Deck:** Top conjunctions (and Top adjectives for *melhor / pior*).

### 4. `alguem-ninguem`: alguém, ninguém, algo, nada, tudo; algum, nenhum, outro (after `object-pronouns`)
- **Teaches:** the pairs *alguém / ninguém*, *alguma coisa (algo) / nada*, *tudo*, *todo mundo* (singular verb); *algum / nenhum*, which agree; *outro* with no *um*; *cada*, which never changes; *todo dia* vs *todo o dia*.
- **English-speaker trap:** *Não tenho algo* for "I don't have anything" (Portuguese says *nada*); *um outro café* for "another coffee"; *todo mundo* read as "the whole world" and given a plural verb.
- **Examples:** *Você quer alguma coisa? Não, nada, obrigado.* · *Tem alguém em casa? Não tem ninguém.* · *Todo mundo gosta de pão de queijo.*
- **Deck:** Top pronouns and Top words.

### Smaller gaps (a section in an existing lesson, not a new lesson)
- **Ordinals** (*primeiro, segundo, terceiro*, which agree): one section in `numbers` (dates: *primeiro de maio*) or in `city` (*a primeira à direita*, floors). *primeiro* is a card in Top adjectives.
- **Comparisons** (*mais… do que, melhor, pior*): covered by proposed lesson 3, or a section in `adjectives`.
