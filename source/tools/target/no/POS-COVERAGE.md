**Key**
- **taught**: a lesson presents it as grammar, with forms and practice.
- **touched**: it shows up inside another lesson's topic, or only part of the class is taught.
- **missing**: no lesson presents it.

## Summary

- **Missing:** interjections; indefinite and relative pronouns (*noe, ingen, alle, som*).
- **Touched only:** adverbs as a class; prepositions beyond fixed chunks; conjunctions, and subordinate-clause word order; comparison of adjectives; ordinals past *tredje*; determiners other than articles, possessives, demonstratives and *mange*.
- **Taught:** nouns, verbs (A1 scope), adjective agreement, articles and definite forms, personal, object, reflexive, possessive and demonstrative pronouns, cardinal numbers.

## Proposed lessons

Outlines only. Each says where it would sit and which core deck it links to.

### 1. `interjections`: Au!, Uff!, Skål!: reacting in Norwegian (after `negation`)
- **Teaches:** 12–15 reactions grouped by feeling: pain and surprise (*Au!, Oi!, Jøss!*), sympathy (*Huff, Stakkars deg!, Så synd!*), annoyance (*Uff!, Søren!, Pokker!*), disgust (*Fy!*), toasting and cheering (*Skål!, Heia!*), agreeing (*Greit!, Jepp!*), and *Å nei! / Å ja!*.
- **English-speaker trap:** carrying *ouch, wow, oops* over; using the subtitle swear words, which are much stronger in Norwegian than they look; *Hæ?* to a stranger (say *Unnskyld?*).
- **Examples:** *Au! Det gjorde vondt.* · *Huff, stakkars deg.* · *Skål for Anna!*
- **Deck:** Top interjections (every card has a dialogue).

### 2. `adverbs`: ikke's neighbours: alltid, aldri, kanskje, også (after `negation`)
- **Teaches:** the words that sit in *ikke*'s slot after the conjugated verb (*alltid, aldri, ofte, også, bare, kanskje, nok, faktisk*); degree words before an adjective (*veldig, ganske, litt, for*); the place / direction pairs *ut – ute, inn – inne, opp – oppe, hjem – hjemme*.
- **English-speaker trap:** *Jeg alltid drikker* (English order); *Jeg er ute* for "I'm going out" (that is *Jeg går ut*); *for* misread as "for" in *for dyrt* (too expensive).
- **Examples:** *Jeg drikker aldri kaffe om kvelden.* · *Barna er ute.* · *Det er for dyrt.*
- **Deck:** Top adverbs.

### 3. `clauses`: at, hvis, fordi, når: joining two ideas (after `past`)
- **Teaches:** *at* after verbs of thinking and saying (often dropped); *hvis* for real conditions; *når* (every time, future) versus *da* (one time in the past); *fordi* and *mens*; *ikke* and *alltid* before the verb inside such a clause; verb first in the main clause when the clause comes first.
- **English-speaker trap:** *fordi jeg kan ikke* (main-clause order inside the clause); *Hvis det regner, jeg blir hjemme* (no verb second after the clause); *når* for a single past event.
- **Examples:** *Jeg tror at det regner.* · *Hvis det regner, blir jeg hjemme.* · *Da jeg var barn, bodde jeg i Bergen.*
- **Deck:** Top conjunctions (*hvis* is not a card yet, because the pron tool gets it wrong; CORE-SOURCES.md, section 5). This lesson goes past the profile's NoW chapters 1–5 scope, so Claude and Kii decide whether it belongs in this course.

### 4. `noe-ingen`: noe, noen, ingen, alle, som (after `object`)
- **Teaches:** *noe* (something) versus *noen* (someone, some); *ingen* and *ingenting*; *alle* and *alt*; *man* for people in general; the relative *som* (*mannen som bor her*); *hvilken / hvilket / hvilke*.
- **English-speaker trap:** *Jeg har ikke noe* versus *Jeg har ingenting* (both fine) but not *Jeg har ikke ingenting*; *noen* for "something"; leaving out *som* where English drops "who".
- **Examples:** *Er det noen hjemme?* · *Det er ingen her.* · *Mannen som bor her, heter Erik.*
- **Deck:** Top pronouns.

### 5. `prepositions`: i, på, om, for … siden: small words of place and time (after `time`)
- **Teaches:** *i* versus *på* for common places (*i Oslo, i butikken, på jobb, på kino, på stasjonen*) as groups, not one rule; *hos* (at someone's place); time: *om en uke* (in a week), *for to år siden* (two years ago), *i to år* (for two years).
- **English-speaker trap:** *i jobb* for *på jobb*; *i en uke* for "in a week" (that is *om en uke*); *for to år* for "two years ago".
- **Examples:** *Jeg er på jobb.* · *Vi ses om en uke.* · *Jeg kom hit for to år siden.*
- **Deck:** Top prepositions. Note: `om` and `hos` are not cards yet because the pron tool gets them wrong (CORE-SOURCES.md, section 5).

### Smaller gaps (a section in an existing lesson, not a new lesson)
- **Comparison** (*større enn, den største, bedre, best*): a section in `adjectives` or `double-def`. Links to Top adjectives.
- **Determiners** (*all / alt / alle, hver, hele, annen, samme, begge, mye* versus *mange*): a section in `plural`. Links to Top words.
- **Ordinals past *tredje*** (*fjerde, femte*, dates like *den femte mai*): a section in `time` or `home`. Links to Top words, which has *femte* (*fjerde* is left out for its pron).
