# Parts of speech in the en-ja lessons

Writer CORE-JA, 2026-11-23. Sources read: every lesson's section headings in `lessons-1.js`, `lessons-2.js` and `lessons-3.js`, and word searches through the lesson bodies, practice items and checks. The searches matched whole words or verb endings, so that たら would not match あたらしい, or まだ やまだ. No lesson was written or changed.

**Key**
- **taught:** a lesson presents it as grammar, with forms and practice.
- **touched:** it comes up inside another lesson's topic, or only part of the class is taught.
- **missing:** no lesson presents it.

| Part of speech | Lessons that teach it (id: title) | How fully | What a learner still cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noun | `desu`: X は Y です; `kosoado`: これ・それ・あれ; `no-mo`: の and も; `noun-mod`: Describing people and things with verbs | taught | Little. Japanese nouns have no articles, gender or required plural, and the lessons say so. Relational nouns (うえ, した, なか, まえ, となり) are taught only for location in `arimasu`, not for time (まえに, あとで). |
| Verb | `masu`, `past`, `invite`, `te`, `te-requests`, `teiru`, `short`, `to-omou`, `tsumori`, `tai`, `koto-aru`, `n-desu`, `must` | taught | Giving and receiving (あげる, くれる, もらう) never appear. The potential form (できる for "can", られる / える forms) is missing, so "I can speak Japanese" is out of reach. Conditionals (〜たら, 〜ば, 〜と) are missing; ば appears only frozen inside なければ いけません (`must`). |
| Adjective | `adjectives`: い and な adjectives; `suki`: すき・きらい; `compare`: より・いちばん; `te` (joining adjectives); `tsumori` (〜く なります) | taught | Turn an adjective into an adverb on its own (はやく たべます, しずかに はなします). The く / に forms appear only before なります. |
| Adverb | No lesson on adverbs as a class. Parts of it: `masu` (よく, ときどき, あまり + ません); `adjectives` ("Very, and not very": とても, あまり); `koto-aru` ("Ever"); time words in `masu` and `past` | touched | Use もう / まだ ("already / not yet") and ぜんぜん + negative: まだ and ぜんぜん never appear in a lesson, and もう only in the set phrase もう いちど (`te-requests`). Use the frequency scale (いつも, よく, ときどき, たまに, ぜんぜん). Make adverbs from adjectives. Soften with ちょっと / けっこう / わりと. The word "adverb" never appears in the lessons. |
| Pronoun | `desu` ("Leave out 'I' and 'you'", さん); `kosoado` (これ / それ / あれ, ここ / そこ / あそこ / どこ, だれの) | taught (demonstratives, the you-rule) / touched (the rest) | Make indefinite and negative words from question words: なにか, だれか, どこか / なにも, だれも + negative (no lesson uses them). Choose a first-person word (わたし, ぼく, おれ). Use the casual こっち / そっち / あっち / どっち. |
| Particle (in place of preposition) | `desu` (は, か); `no-mo` (の, も); `particles` (を, に, へ, で); `arimasu` (に, が); `numbers` (に with times); `suki` (が); `compare` (より); `to-omou` (quoting と); `n-desu` (から as "because", one line) | taught | から / まで for "from … until" are never taught (から appears as a wrong option in `compare`, in これから in `tsumori`, and as "because" in one line of `n-desu`). と for "with" appears in examples (`past`, `noun-mod`) but is never explained; や, だけ, しか and ぐらい never appear. The sentence-final ね and よ turn up in set phrases (いいですね in `invite`, ほうが いいですよ in `must`) but are never explained. |
| Conjunction (connectors) | `te` (the て form joins actions); `n-desu` (ので); `to-omou` (と おもいます) | touched | Start a sentence with でも, だから, それから, じゃあ. Join two clauses with 〜けど / 〜が ("but") or 〜とき ("when"). Use 〜まえに / 〜たあとで. Use the everyday conditional 〜たら. |
| Interjection | `invite` (ちょっと… as a soft no); `short` (うん, ううん); greetings in `desu` | touched | Most reactions (へえ, ええ, あのう, えーと, なるほど, しまった, よかった) are missing, so learners cannot sound natural while listening (あいづち), which matters a lot in Japanese conversation. |
| Article / determiner | `kosoado` ("これ alone, この with a noun": この / その / あの / どの) | taught | こんな / そんな / あんな / どんな ("this kind of …") are missing; どんな appears only in the deck card どんな えいがが すきですか. |

The new `Top counters` deck covers the words, but no lesson covers the sound changes. |

## Proposed lessons

**1. もう・まだ・ぜんぜん: adverbs of time, frequency and degree**
- Teaches: もう + past (already), まだ + ていません (not yet), the frequency scale いつも → よく → ときどき → たまに → あまり / ぜんぜん + negative, and adverbs made from adjectives (はやく, しずかに).
- Trap: English "I still haven't eaten" is まだ たべて いません, not まだ たべませんでした. And ぜんぜん needs a negative.
- Examples: もう たべました。 / まだ たべて いません。 / ぜんぜん わかりません。
- Core deck: Top adverbs.

**2. でも・だから・けど: joining sentences**
- Teaches: sentence-initial でも, だから, それから, じゃあ; clause-joining 〜けど / 〜が; 〜とき; 〜まえに / 〜たあとで.
- Trap: English puts "but" in the middle. でも starts a new sentence, and inside one sentence Japanese uses けど. "When I was a child" puts とき after the clause.
- Examples: たかいです。でも、おいしいです。 / たかいけど、おいしいです。 / ねる まえに シャワーを あびます。
- Core deck: Top connectors.

**3. から・まで・と・だけ: more particles, and ね / よ**
- Teaches: から … まで (from … until), と (with; and), や (and so on), だけ / しか + negative (only), and the sentence-final ね (agreement) and よ (new information).
- Trap: English "only" is one word. Japanese has だけ with a positive verb and しか with a negative one. Too much よ sounds pushy.
- Examples: くじから ごじまで はたらきます。 / ともだちと いきます。 / せんえんしか ありません。
- Core deck: Top particles.

**4. なにか・だれも: someone, nothing, anywhere**
- Teaches: question word + か (なにか, だれか, どこか, いつか) and question word + も + negative (なにも, だれも, どこも).
- Trap: English "anything" works in both questions and negatives. Japanese uses なにか in questions and なにも + negative in negatives. なにも たべました is wrong.
- Examples: なにか のみますか。 / なにも いりません。 / だれも いません。
- Core deck: Top pronouns.

**5. へえ・なるほど・えーと: reacting like a Japanese speaker (あいづち)**
- Teaches: listening noises and reactions: ええ, へえ, そうですか, なるほど, ほんとうですか, えーと, あのう, よかった, しまった; when each one is polite.
- Trap: Silence while listening sounds cold in Japanese. And なるほど said to a boss can sound like you are judging them.
- Examples: へえ、そうですか。 / えーと、ちょっと まって ください。 / よかった！
- Core deck: Top interjections.

**6. あげる・くれる・もらう: giving and receiving** (optional, A2)
- Teaches: あげる (I give out), くれる (someone gives me), もらう (I receive), with に.
- Trap: English "give" covers both directions. Japanese picks the verb by who receives.
- Examples: ははに はなを あげました。 / ともだちが ほんを くれました。 / ともだちに ほんを もらいました。
- Core deck: Top verbs.
