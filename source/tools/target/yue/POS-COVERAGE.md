# en-yue: parts of speech in the lessons

Writer EN-YUE-T, 2026-11-23. The 27 lessons (`lessons-1.js` … `lessons-3.js`) were checked by their headings and by searching for the key words. "Taught" means the lesson explains it as grammar and has practice items on it. "Touched" means it appears, but the class is not explained. Nothing below has been written as a lesson.

## Proposed lessons

**1. Linking ideas: 因為…所以…, 但係, 如果…就…**
- Teaches: the clause-linking conjunctions 因為, 所以, 但係 / 不過, 如果…就…, 然後 / 跟住, and 嘅時候 for "when".
- English-speaker trap: putting the "when" or "if" clause last as in English. Cantonese puts it first: 如果落雨，我就唔去. Also leaving out 就 in the second half.
- Examples: 我唔去，因為我好攰。 / 好靚，不過好貴。 / 如果落雨，我哋就唔去。
- Links to: Top conjunctions.

**2. Reacting: 好嘢！ 係咩？ 死喇！**
- Teaches: about a dozen everyday reactions and when each fits: surprise (係咩, 唔係嘛), joy (好嘢), trouble (死喇), agreement (係囉), encouragement (加油).
- English-speaker trap: answering news with a flat 好 or 係, which sounds cold. Also translating "wow" or "really?" word for word.
- Examples: 好嘢！恭喜你！ / 係咩？我唔知喎！ / 死喇，我唔記得咗！
- Links to: Top interjections.

**3. With, to, for: coverbs 同, 畀, 幫, 用, 對**
- Teaches: the coverb goes before the main verb with its object, just as 喺 + place does: 同朋友去, 畀錢佢, 幫我睇吓, 用筷子食.
- English-speaker trap: putting "with you" or "for me" at the end, as in English (我去同你).
- Examples: 我同朋友去飲茶。 / 你可唔可以幫我影相呀？ / 佢對我好好。
- Links to: Top coverbs and place words.

**4. Time adverbs: 就, 仲, 先, 再, 又, 已經**
- Teaches: the short adverbs that go before the verb, and sentence-final 先 ("first").
- English-speaker trap: 再 vs 又 (再 for the future, 又 for something that has happened again), and putting "first" before the verb (先 often comes last: 你食先).
- Examples: 我仲未食飯。 / 唔該再講一次。 / 今日又落雨。
- Links to: Top adverbs.

**5. Everyone, nobody, anything: 自己, 大家, 有人, 冇人, 咩都**
- Teaches: reflexive 自己, inclusive 大家, and the indefinites built from question words + 都 (咩都, 邊個都).
- English-speaker trap: for "nobody" or "anyone", reaching for a separate word instead of building it from 冇人 or 邊個都.
- Examples: 我自己去得喇。 / 冇人知。 / 我咩都食。
- Links to: Top pronouns.

**6. More particles, and the verb endings 住, 吓, 晒, 埋**
- Teaches: active use of 喎, 囉, 咩, 㗎 and 咋, and the verb endings 住 (keep doing), 吓 (briefly), 晒 (all) and 埋 (also, finish off).
- English-speaker trap: leaving every sentence bare, which sounds curt, or putting 晒 before the verb (晒 comes after it: 食晒).
- Examples: 十蚊咋。 / 你試吓先啦。 / 我食晒喇。
- Links to: Top particles.
