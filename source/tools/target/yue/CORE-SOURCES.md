## 2. Classes fitted to Cantonese (brief rule 2)

- **Top prepositions → `Top coverbs and place words`.** Cantonese has no prepositions. Their work is done by coverbs: verbs used before the main verb, such as 同 (with), 畀 (to, for), 幫 (for), 用 (with a tool), 對 (towards) and 經 (via). Place words come after the noun: 枱上面, 商場入面. Both kinds are in this one deck. 喺 is the main coverb, but it is already a card (Tone twins).
- **Added `Top measure words`.** Every counted or pointed-at noun needs one. The course's Classifiers deck teaches them only inside phrases (一隻貓, 三本書). This deck gives each one as a card of its own (個, 隻, 本, 張 …), because no existing card has a bare measure word as its target. 對 (pair) went to the coverbs deck as "towards", and its classifier use is in the note.
- **Added `Top particles`.** The launching brief asked for sentence-final 呀, 啦, 喎, 囉, 咩, 㗎 and 呢 and the aspect markers 咗, 緊, 過 and 住. 咗 is already a card (First characters), so it was skipped. The deck also has 吖, 喇, 嘛, 咋, 啩, 噃, 嗎 and the verb suffixes 吓, 晒, 埋 and 添.
- **Adjectives**: kept as a deck. The first card's note says that Cantonese adjectives work like verbs and take no 係.
- **Top words**: Cantonese has no articles. That role is filled by the measure word (本書 = the book) and by 呢 / 嗰 + measure word. 嗰, 每, 所有 and 第 are in Top words. 呢 is a single card (in Top particles), so "this" is taught through 呢個 and 呢啲 in Top pronouns.
- **Words with two jobs**, one card each: 過 (experiential marker; also "than": both in the note, card in particles), 住 (aspect; also "to live": particles), 咩 (surprised-question particle; "what" is taught as 咩嘢 in pronouns), 幾 (quite: adverbs; "how many" in the note), 吓 (do-briefly suffix; the interjection "huh?" haa2 cannot be a separate card with the same characters), 幫 (help / for someone: coverbs; the verb is taught as 幫手), 到 (verbs, with "until" in the note).
- No Mandarin-only words. Hong Kong forms throughout: 沖涼 (not 洗澡), 生果, 揸車, 閂, 攰, 瞓覺, 睇, 嘢. Written-register words that Hong Kong people do say carry a note pointing to the spoken one (價錢 → 幾多錢, 需要 → 要, 亦 → 都, 會議 → 開會). 東西 and 上班 were dropped as too Mandarin-flavoured for speech.

## 4. Flirting and Compliments

- Built from `docs/plans/compliments-yue.md`. Deck 2 of that plan became the phrase and move layers of `Flirting`. Deck 1 became `Compliments`, extended to 28 cards.
- Hong Kong flirting is fairly indirect. It usually starts with an invitation to eat or have coffee (一齊食飯吖？), and 我鍾意你 is a real confession. The notes say this. 我愛你 is a word card with a "heavy" warning, not a phrase.
- There are four kind ways to say no, including the culturally standard 你係好人，不過… ("you're a good person, but…").
- Plan cards dropped because the tool cannot generate their Jyutping: 你好靚女呀 (the tool reads 女 as neoi5, not neoi2), 好主意, 你好勤力, 你好搞笑, 你好醒目, 呢件衫好襯你, 張相影得好靚, 過獎喇, 我好掛住你, 你今個週末得唔得閒呀, and 我請你飲咖啡吖 (the tool reads 請 as cing2; "to treat" is ceng2). The plan's 我冇呢個意思 was dropped as ambiguous, as the plan itself warned.

## 6. For the lead

- **Measure words:** bare 個, 本, 杯, 張, 隻, 部, 架, 件, 條 and 碗 are cards in `Top measure words`, although the `classifiers` lesson and the Classifiers deck teach them inside phrases. No existing card has them as a bare target, so nothing clashes. If "already taught" was meant as "taught in a lesson", cut those ten cards. The other twenty are new.
- **Ten cards a native speaker should check first:**
  1. 哦 o4 = "Oh, I see." Is it o4 or o6 for understanding?
  2. 唔好意思，我唔想拍拖住。 Is sentence-final 住 ("for now") natural here?
  3. 你識講廣東話㗎？ Is 㗎 natural for the surprised question, or is 咩 better?
  4. 你係好人，不過我哋做朋友啦。 Does this read as a kind no?
  5. 帥 seoi3 as everyday Hong Kong praise, compared with 好有型.
  6. 時間 si4 gaan3 (the tool's reading; si4 gaan1 is also given in dictionaries).
  7. 樓 lau2 = flat / floor (as a building, 樓 is lau4).
  8. 喎 wo3 = noticing (好貴喎！), against 噃 bo3 = reminder (好貴噃): are the two distinguished well?
  9. 冇眼睇 as "I can't watch!", and 係噉先 as "that's it for now".
  10. 經 ging1 as a coverb (呢架巴士經唔經旺角呀？).
