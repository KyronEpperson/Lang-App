# Parts of speech in the en-vi lessons

Writer CORE-VI, 2026-11-23. Method: the bodies of 10 lessons were read (`pronouns, this-that, adjectives, who-what, classifiers, family, verbs, location, because, requests`); for all 28 lessons, whole-word searches ran over the title, body, check questions and practice items, and each hit used below was read in context. The deck cards and extras were searched too. Classifiers and sentence particles get their own rows because Vietnamese grammar depends on them. No lesson was written or changed.

**Key**
- **taught**: a lesson presents it as grammar, with forms and practice.
- **touched**: it appears inside another lesson's topic, or only part of the class is taught.
- **missing**: no lesson presents it.

## Summary

- **Missing:** interjections; attitude particles (*nhỉ, à, chứ, cơ, kìa*).
- **Touched only:** conjunctions beyond *vì … nên* (no *và, nếu … thì, khi, để, tuy … nhưng*); adverbs as a class (no *cũng, vẫn, lại, nữa, đều, thường*); prepositions of source and purpose (*từ, cho, theo, về*); the rest of the pronoun system (*mình, họ, nhau, mọi người*, indefinites); determiners (*các / những, mỗi, mọi*); ordinals.
- **Taught:** nouns, verbs (A1 scope), adjectives and comparison, kinship pronouns, demonstratives, place prepositions, classifiers (the core set), cardinal numbers, politeness particles.

## Proposed lessons

Each outline links to the new core decks. The placement is after the lessons each one builds on.

### 1. `va-neu-khi`: và, nếu … thì, khi, để: joining ideas (after `because`)
- **Teaches:** *và* (and, between words and clauses); *nếu … thì* (if … then, with *thì* marking the result); *khi* (when) against the question *khi nào?*; *để* + verb (in order to); *trước khi / sau khi* + verb; *tuy … nhưng* (although … but, both halves marked like *vì … nên*); *hoặc* (or) in statements against *hay* in questions.
- **English-speaker trap:** leaving out *thì* or *nhưng* because English uses one word; putting *để* after *muốn* (*Tôi muốn để ăn*); using *hay* for "or" in a statement, where *hoặc* is clearer; *khi nào* inside a statement.
- **Examples:** *Nếu mai trời mưa thì tôi ở nhà.* · *Tôi học tiếng Việt để nói chuyện với bà.* · *Tuy đắt nhưng rất ngon.*
- **Deck:** Top conjunctions.

### 2. `cung-van-lai`: cũng, vẫn, lại, nữa, đều and how often (after `verbs`)
- **Teaches:** the slot between subject and verb, shared with *đã / đang / sẽ*: *cũng* (also), *vẫn* (still), *đều* (both, all), *vừa / mới* (just), *sắp* (about to), *từng* (ever); *lại* and *nữa* after the verb (*nói lại*, *một bát nữa*, *không … nữa*); frequency (*thường, hay, luôn, thỉnh thoảng, ít khi, không bao giờ*).
- **English-speaker trap:** putting "also" at the end (*Tôi thích cũng*); *nữa* before the noun; doubling *không bao giờ* with another *không*.
- **Examples:** *Tôi cũng là sinh viên.* · *Mười giờ rồi mà anh ấy vẫn ngủ.* · *Tôi không bao giờ uống rượu.*
- **Deck:** Top adverbs.

### 3. `oi-troi-oi`: Ôi!, Trời ơi!, Thôi chết!: reacting in Vietnamese (after `requests`)
- **Teaches:** 12–15 exclamations grouped by feeling: surprise and pleasure (*Ôi!, Ồ!, Chà!*), pain (*Ối!, Á!*), mistakes (*Thôi chết!*), shock or annoyance (*Trời ơi!*, Hanoi *Ôi giời ơi!*), relief (*Hú hồn!, May quá!*), sympathy (*Tiếc quá!*), cheering (*Cố lên!, Hoan hô!, Chúc mừng!*), warnings (*Cẩn thận!*), the phone *A lô*.
- **English-speaker trap:** carrying over *ouch, oops, wow*; *Ê!* or *Hả?* to an older person (use *Anh ơi!* and *Dạ?*); reading *Thôi chết!* ("dead!") as grim.
- **Examples:** *Ối, đau quá!* · *Thôi chết, em quên ví rồi!* · *Hú hồn! Em có sao không?*
- **Deck:** Top interjections (every card already has a dialogue).

### 4. `nhi-a-chu`: nhỉ, à, chứ, thôi, cơ: the words that add feeling (after `requests`)
- **Teaches:** the attitude particles at the end of a sentence: *nhỉ* (inviting agreement), *à / ư* (surprise or checking), *chứ* (of course; "you are, aren't you?"), *thôi* (only, that's all), *đấy* (pointing it out), *cơ* (insisting), *kìa* (look), *vậy / thế* in questions, *đi* (let's / go ahead), and *cái đã* (first).
- **English-speaker trap:** treating every particle as a question mark; *nhỉ* to someone who cannot agree (it asks for agreement); stacking *ạ* and a casual particle.
- **Examples:** *Hôm nay trời đẹp nhỉ?* · *Anh về rồi à?* · *Được chứ!*
- **Deck:** Top particles.

### 5. `minh-ho-nhau`: mình, họ, nhau, mọi người; someone and nobody (after `family`)
- **Teaches:** *mình* (I among friends; "we, you and me"), *chúng mình*; *họ* (they) and the friendlier *mọi người*; *người ta* (people in general); *nhau* (each other: *yêu nhau, gặp nhau*); *tự* (oneself); the indefinites *ai đó, gì đó* and negatives *không ai, không … gì*; *ai / gì / đâu + cũng* (everyone, everything, everywhere); possession with *của* (*của tôi* = mine).
- **English-speaker trap:** *họ* for a polite "they" about elders (use *các bác, các cô*); *nó* for a person; English word order in *Không ai biết* (Vietnamese puts *không* first).
- **Examples:** *Mình đi ăn phở đi!* · *Hai người yêu nhau.* · *Không ai ở nhà.*
- **Deck:** Top pronouns.

### Smaller gaps (a section in an existing lesson, not a new lesson)
- **từ, cho, theo, về as prepositions:** a section in `location` or `motion`: *từ … đến*, *mua quà cho mẹ*, *theo tôi*, *nói về*. Linked to Top prepositions.
- **các / những, mỗi, mọi:** a section in `classifiers` (plural noun phrases: *các bạn, những quyển sách này, mỗi ngày*). Linked to Top words.
- **Ordinals** (*thứ nhất, thứ hai…*, *lần thứ hai*) and the "second / Monday" clash: a section in `time`. Linked to Top words.
- **More classifiers** (*quả, tờ, bức, tấm, căn, ngôi, chuyến*): a table added to `classifiers`. Linked to Top classifiers.
