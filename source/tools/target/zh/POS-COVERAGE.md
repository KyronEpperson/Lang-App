# Parts of speech in the en-zh lessons

Writer CORE-ZH, 2026-11-23. Read from `lessons-1.js`, `lessons-2.js` and `lessons-3.js`: every lesson's title and section headings, plus word searches across all lesson bodies. No lesson was written or changed.

**Key:** **taught** = a lesson presents it as grammar, with practice; **touched** = it appears inside another lesson's topic, or only part of the class is taught; **missing** = no lesson presents it.

## Summary

- **Missing:** interjections; complements (degree, result, direction, potential); the particles 过, 着, 得, 是…的.
- **Touched only:** coverbs (only 在 is taught); conjunctions (only 和, 还是, 或者); adverbs beyond 也 / 都 / 还 / 很 / 太; comparison with 比; determiners (no "Chinese has no articles" lesson); ordinals.
- **Taught:** nouns, core verbs, adjectives as verbs, pronouns, 在 + location words, the particles 吗 / 呢 / 的 / 了 / 吧, measure words, cardinal numbers.

## Proposed lessons

1. **Coverbs: 给, 跟, 从…到, 离, 往, 用**
   - Teaches: Chinese "prepositions" are verbs placed before the main verb; who / when / where / with whom / how all come before it.
   - Trap: English puts "to, for, with" after the verb (call to you, eat with chopsticks).
   - Examples: 我给你打电话。 · 我从美国来。 · 我用筷子吃饭。
   - Links: Top prepositions (coverbs).
2. **Joining sentences: 因为…所以, 但是, 如果…就, …的时候**
   - Teaches: paired connectors, 就 before the second verb, 的时候 after the clause.
   - Trap: 和 cannot join sentences; "when" goes at the end of the clause, not the start.
   - Examples: 因为下雨，所以我们不去了。 · 如果你有空，我们就一起去。 · 我吃饭的时候不看手机。
   - Links: Top conjunctions.
3. **How well and with what result: complements**
   - Teaches: verb + 得 + adjective (说得很好), result complements (看见, 听懂, 吃完), and can / can't (听不懂).
   - Trap: English "speaks Chinese well" → 中文说得很好 (the verb comes again or the object goes first), never *说中文很好.
   - Examples: 他说得很快。 · 我听不懂。 · 我吃完了。
   - Links: Top verbs (notes), Top particles (得).
4. **过 and 着: have you ever, and a state that lasts**
   - Teaches: verb + 过 for experience, 没 + verb + 过 for "never", verb + 着 for a lasting state.
   - Trap: 过 is not a past tense, and 了 is not "have ever".
   - Examples: 你去过北京吗？ · 我没吃过北京烤鸭。 · 门开着。
   - Links: Top particles.
5. **Comparing: 比, 一样, 更, 最**
   - Teaches: A 比 B + adjective; A 跟 B 一样; 更 and 最.
   - Trap: no 很 after 比 (*我比他很高); the adjective does not change form.
   - Examples: 今天比昨天冷。 · 我的手机跟你的一样。 · 这个更好。
   - Links: Top adjectives, Top prepositions (coverbs).
6. **Reacting: 我的天, 加油, 是吗, 不会吧, 算了**
   - Teaches: everyday exclamations and when each fits; 喂 on the phone; 啊 to sound warm.
   - Trap: English "oh" and "wow" have no single Chinese twin; 加油 is not "add oil" but "go for it".
   - Examples: 加油！你一定可以！ · 不会吧！他才二十岁！ · 算了，我们回家吃吧。
   - Links: Top interjections, Top particles (啊).
7. **Time adverbs: 就, 才, 已经, 再, 又**
   - Teaches: 就 (sooner than expected) vs 才 (later than expected); 已经…了; 再 (again, future) vs 又 (again, past).
   - Trap: English "again" is one word; Chinese splits it by time.
   - Examples: 他十点才起床。 · 我已经吃饭了。 · 请再说一遍。
   - Links: Top adverbs.
