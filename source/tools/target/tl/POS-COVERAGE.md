## Proposed lessons

Note: the lessons below use some words that are not yet cards in the linked decks. *kaya* (so), *lagi*, *Naku!*, *Aray!*, *Hala!*, *mula sa*, *tungkol sa* and *sa harap / likod ng* are left out until `respell.js` LEX lists them; CORE-SOURCES.md §4 gives the LEX entry each one needs.

**1. Joining ideas: at, pero, kasi, kung, kaya** (conjunctions)
- Teaches: *at* / *o*; *pero*; *kasi* (spoken) vs *dahil* (careful) for "because"; *kung* "if", *kapag* "when(ever)"; *kaya* "so"; *habang* "while".
- English-speaker trap: using *kung* for "when" in the past (Tagalog uses *nang* or *noong*), and starting a sentence with *Dahil…* then forgetting the main clause.
- Examples: *Gusto ko, pero wala akong pera.* / *Hindi ako sumama kasi pagod ako.* / *Kung gusto mo, sumama ka.*
- Links to: Top conjunctions.

**2. Reacting like a Filipino: Naku!, Uy!, Sayang!, Hala!** (interjections)
- Teaches: the common reactions, what each one means, and who you can say it to. *Hoy!* is rude to strangers, *Psst!* is rude to elders, and *Susmaryosep!* is used by older speakers.
- English-speaker trap: using *Hoy!* to call a waiter or an elder. Use *Kuya / Ate* or *Excuse me po* instead.
- Examples: *Sayang! Sarado na ang tindahan.* / *Hala! Nasa jeep yata ang cellphone ko.* / *Teka, kukunin ko lang ang bag ko.*
- Links to: Top interjections (the deck still lacks *Naku!* and *Aray!*, because of the pron tool).

**3. How often and how much: lagi, minsan, masyado, medyo, nang** (adverbs)
- Teaches: frequency (*lagi, minsan, araw-araw*), degree (*masyado, medyo, sobra, halos*) and manner (*nang + describing word*). Covers where each goes: frequency words often come first and take the linker (*Lagi siyang late*).
- English-speaker trap: putting the adverb at the end as in English (*Late siya lagi* is possible, but the first position with a linker is the normal pattern), and forgetting the linker in *masyadong mahal*.
- Examples: *Lagi siyang late.* / *Masyadong mahal ito.* / *Medyo malayo ang bahay namin.*
- Links to: Top adverbs.

**4. Where things are: sa ilalim ng, sa tabi ng, para sa, tungkol sa** (the sa phrases that do the work of prepositions)
- Teaches: *nasa / sa* + location noun + *ng* (*ilalim, ibabaw, tabi, loob, labas, harap, likod*); *para sa* "for", *tungkol sa* "about", *mula sa* "from".
- English-speaker trap: translating "on the table" as *sa mesa* every time. That works, but "under" and "next to" need the location noun and *ng*: *sa ilalim ng mesa*, not *ilalim mesa*.
- Examples: *Nasa ilalim ng mesa ang pusa.* / *Umupo ka sa tabi ng lola mo.* / *Para sa iyo ang regalo.*
- Links to: Top markers and particles.
