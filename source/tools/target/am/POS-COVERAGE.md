**Key:** taught = a lesson presents it as grammar, with forms and practice. touched = it shows up inside another topic, or only part of the class is taught. missing = no lesson presents it.

## Summary

- **Missing:** adverbs, conjunctions, interjections, postpositions other than ወደ, ordinal numbers.
- **Touched only:** adjectives as a class.
- **Taught:** nouns, verbs (A1 scope), pronouns (subject, demonstrative, possessive endings), the prefix prepositions, the definite ending, cardinal numbers.
- Every missing lesson also needs lexicon lines first: the words it would teach are not in `lexicon.txt` (see CORE-SOURCES.md section 5).

## Proposed lessons (outlines only)

### 1. `adverbs`: now, here, always, and very (after `likes`)
- **Teaches:** time (አሁን, ዛሬ, ነገ, ትናንት), place (እዚህ, እዛ), frequency (ሁልጊዜ), degree (በጣም, ትንሽ); position: before the verb, which stays last.
- **Trap:** putting the adverb after the verb as in English ("I drink coffee always").
- **Examples:** አሁን እሄዳለሁ (I'm going now). · እዚህ ቡና አለ (There's coffee here). · ሁልጊዜ ሻይ እጠጣለሁ (I always drink tea).
- **Deck:** Top adverbs (to be built once the words are in the lexicon).

### 2. `connectors`: and, but, because, so (after `questions`)
- **Teaches:** እና / -ና between nouns, ግን, ወይም / ወይስ (statement or question), ምክንያቱም, ስለዚህ, ከዚያ; the -ም "also" ending.
- **Trap:** using እና to join two full sentences the way English uses "and"; Amharic often chains verbs instead.
- **Examples:** ቡና እና ሻይ (coffee and tea). · ሻይ እወዳለሁ ግን ቡና አልወድም (I like tea but not coffee). · ደህና ነኝ ምክንያቱም … (I'm fine because …).
- **Deck:** Top conjunctions (to be built) and Questions and connectors.

### 3. `reactions`: ኧረ, ወይ, እንዴ (after `greetings`)
- **Teaches:** the eight or so sounds people react with: modesty (ኧረ), surprise (ወይ, እንዴ), dismay (ወይኔ), joy (እሰይ), praise (ጎበዝ), and the reply እግዚአብሔር ይመስገን.
- **Trap:** carrying "wow", "oops", "ouch" over from English; answering a compliment with a flat "thank you" where ኧረ is expected.
- **Examples:** ኧረ አይደለም! (Oh no, not really!) · ወይ! ውድ ነው (Oh my, it's expensive). · ጎበዝ! (Well done!)
- **Deck:** Top interjections (to be built); Flirting already uses ኧረ.

### 4. `with-on-in`: ከ … ጋር, ላይ, ውስጥ (after `prepositions`)
- **Teaches:** the frame prefix + noun + postposition: ከ … ጋር (with), … ላይ (on), … ውስጥ (in), … ስር (under), with በ- often added.
- **Trap:** looking for one word before the noun, as in English; leaving off the ከ- of ከ … ጋር in writing.
- **Examples:** ከእናቴ ጋር (with my mother). · ጠረጴዛ ላይ (on the table). · ቤት ውስጥ (in the house).
- **Deck:** Top prepositions and postpositions.

### 5. `adjectives`: before the noun, and the woman form (after `definite`)
- **Teaches:** adjective before the noun (ትልቅ ቤት) versus after it with the copula (ቤቱ ትልቅ ነው); the -ት woman form on nationality words (አሜሪካዊ / አሜሪካዊት); a short list of colours.
- **Trap:** expecting every adjective to change for a woman; most do not, and the verb ending carries gender.
- **Examples:** ቀይ መኪና አለኝ (I have a red car). · እሷ አሜሪካዊት ናት (She is American). · ትልቅ ቤት ነው (It's a big house).
- **Deck:** Top adjectives.
