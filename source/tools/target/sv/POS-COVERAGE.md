# Parts of speech in the en-sv lessons

Writer EN-SV-CORE, 2026-11-23. Read from `lessons-1.js`, `lessons-2.js` and `lessons-3.js`: every lesson body (headings, explanations and `{{ex:}}` rows) for the lessons named below, plus keyword searches across all 26 lessons. No lesson was written or changed.

**Key**
- **taught**: a lesson presents it as grammar, with forms and practice.
- **touched**: it appears inside another lesson's topic, or only part of the class is taught.
- **missing**: no lesson presents it.

## Summary

- **Missing:** interjections; ordinal numbers and dates.
- **Touched only:** adverbs as a class (above all the motion/location pairs `hem / hemma`, `ut / ute`); prepositions as a class (`i` vs `på`, `hos`, time prepositions); subordinating conjunctions other than `att`; indefinite, negative, relative and demonstrative pronouns.
- **Taught:** nouns, verbs (A1 scope), adjectives, articles and the definite suffix, personal and possessive pronouns, cardinal numbers.

## Proposed lessons

Outlines only; none is written. Each links to a Core words deck.

### 1. `interjections`: Oj, aj, usch: reacting in Swedish (after `questions`)
- **Teaches:** 12–15 reactions grouped by feeling: surprise (`Oj!`, `Nämen!`, `Jaha.`), pain and slips (`Aj!`, `Ojdå!`, `Hoppsan!`), dislike (`Usch!`, `Fy!`), brushing off (`Äsch!`), joy (`Toppen!`, `Härligt!`, `Heja!`), rituals (`Skål!`, `Prosit!`, `Lycka till!`); casual `Tjena!` vs `Hej!`.
- **English-speaker trap:** carrying over "ouch", "oops", "wow"; `Va?` to a stranger instead of `Förlåt?`; reading `Fy!` as mild (it scolds).
- **Examples:** `Aj! Min hand!` · `Äsch, det gör inget.` · `Nämen hej, Anna!`
- **Deck:** Top interjections (every card has a dialogue).

### 2. `hem-hemma`: Going home, being at home: place adverbs (after `directions`)
- **Teaches:** the motion/location pairs `hem / hemma`, `ut / ute`, `in / inne`, `upp / uppe`, `ner / nere`, `bort / borta`, `hit / här`, `dit / där`; which verbs take which (`gå, åka, komma` take the motion form; `vara, bo, sitta` take the location form); `vart` (where to) vs `var` (where).
- **English-speaker trap:** English "home", "out", "there" serve both; learners say `Jag är hem` or `Jag går hemma`, and `Var ska du?` for "where are you going".
- **Examples:** `Jag går hem nu.` · `Är du hemma?` · `Vart ska du? Jag ska ut.`
- **Deck:** Top adverbs (with `vart` from Top words).

### 3. `bisats`: Joining sentences: om, när, innan, eftersom-type clauses (after `att`)
- **Teaches:** subordinating `om` (if), `när` (when, not a question here), `innan`, `medan`, `tills`, `så att`, `för att`, `därför att`, `även om`, `trots att`; the one rule that `inte` and `alltid` go before the verb in any of these clauses (extending the `att` rule); a fronted clause counts as the first element, so the main verb comes next (`Om du vill, kan vi gå.`).
- **English-speaker trap:** `Om du vill, vi kan gå` (verb second lost after a fronted clause); `...om han kommer inte` (main-clause `inte` order kept inside the clause); `när` vs `då` for a past single event.
- **Examples:** `Om det regnar, stannar jag hemma.` · `Ring mig innan du går.` · `Han jobbar trots att han inte mår bra.`
- **Deck:** Top conjunctions.

### 4. `någon-ingen`: someone, no one, something, nothing; som (after `possessives`)
- **Teaches:** `någon / något / några` and `ingen / inget / inga` as pronouns and as determiners, agreeing like `en / ett / plural`; `allt / alla`; generic `man`; the relative `som`; demonstratives `den här / det här / de här` and `den där` with the definite suffix on the noun.
- **English-speaker trap:** `Jag har inte något` vs `Jag har inget` (both fine, but not `Jag inte har något`); `den här bil` without the suffix; `man` confused with `en man` (a man).
- **Examples:** `Är det någon här? Nej, det är ingen här.` · `Jag har en vän som bor i Lund.` · `Den här bilen är ny.`
- **Deck:** Top pronouns.

### 5. `i-pa-hos`: i, på, hos, till, från: places and time (after `directions`)
- **Teaches:** `i` vs `på` for places (city, country, building, event: `i Malmö`, `på jobbet`, `på fest`); `hos` for someone's home; `till` / `från`; time: `på måndag`, `i två år`, `om en vecka` (in a week), `för en vecka sedan` (a week ago).
- **English-speaker trap:** `i jobbet` for "at work"; `för två år` for "for two years" (Swedish: `i två år`); `hos` missing (`på Anna` for "at Anna's").
- **Examples:** `Vi äter middag hos Anna.` · `Jag har bott här i två år.` · `Vi ses om en vecka.`
- **Deck:** Top prepositions.

### Smaller gaps (a section in an existing lesson, not a new lesson)
- **Ordinals and dates** (`första, andra, tredje`; `den första maj`): a section in `numbers`. Links to Top words. Months would need the pron tool's word list extended first (CORE-SOURCES.md §6).
- **Comparison** (`större än`, `bättre`, `bäst`): a section in `adjectives`. Links to Top adjectives (`bättre`, `bäst`) and Top conjunctions (`än`).
- **`det finns`** (there is / there are): a short section in `present` or `cafe`. Links to Top verbs (`finnas`).
