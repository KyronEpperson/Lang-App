## Summary

- **Missing:** conjunctions; interjections; future and negative-past / negative-future verbs (سَوْفَ, لَن, لَم); object endings on verbs and most prepositions; relative pronouns; ordinals.
- **Touched only:** prepositions as a class (no lesson, pieces in five lessons); demonstratives beyond هٰذَا / هٰذِهِ; adverbs beyond time words; determiners (كُلّ, بَعْض).
- **Taught:** nouns, present and past verbs, adjectives, independent pronouns and possessive endings, the article and definiteness, cardinal numbers 0-100.

## Proposed lessons

Outlines only; places are suggestions after the lessons each one needs.

### 1. `conjunctions`: and, but, because, if (after `verb-sentences`)
- **Teaches:** وَ and فَ joined to the next word; لٰكِن, أَوْ / أَم (or, in questions), ثُمَّ, لِأَنَّ (with an ending: لِأَنِّي), إِذَا (real if) vs لَوْ (unreal if), عِنْدَمَا vs the question مَتَى; أَن + present verb (أُرِيد أَن أَذْهَب) vs أَنَّ + noun.
- **English-speaker trap:** writing وَ as a separate word with a space; using مَتَى for "when" in a statement; leaving out أَن after "want / can" (أُرِيد أَذْهَب).
- **Examples:** أُرِيد أَن أَذْهَب، لٰكِن لَيْسَ عِنْدِي وَقْت. · لَا أَشْرَب الْقَهْوَة لِأَنَّهَا قَوِيَّة. · عِنْدَمَا أَصِل، أَتَّصِل بِكَ.
- **Deck:** Top conjunctions (19 example rows ready).

### 2. `object-endings`: me, you, him: endings on verbs and prepositions (after `have`)
- **Teaches:** the possessive endings again, now on a verb ("me" is ـنِي, not ـي: يَفْهَمُنِي) and on prepositions (لِي, لَكَ / لَكِ, مَعِي, مِنِّي, عَلَيَّ "I have to"); لِي = "mine" (هٰذَا لِي); the plural ـكُم and ـهُم.
- **English-speaker trap:** using ـي on a verb (يَفْهَمِي); using the independent pronoun as an object (يُحِبّ أَنَا); mixing ـكَ / ـكِ.
- **Examples:** هٰذِهِ الْقَهْوَة لَكَ. · هَل تَأْتِي مَعِي؟ · عَلَيَّ أَن أَعْمَل غَدًا.
- **Deck:** Top prepositions (the 16 suffixed forms) and Top pronouns (the ending cards).

### 3. `future-negation`: will, won't, didn't: سَوْفَ, لَن, لَم (after `past`)
- **Teaches:** سَوْفَ / سَـ + present for the future; لَن + present for "won't"; لَم + present for "didn't" (the past-tense lesson's recognition item); the "I" forms first (سَأَذْهَب, لَن أَذْهَب, لَم أَذْهَب); قَد / لَقَد for recognition.
- **English-speaker trap:** putting لَم before a past verb (لَم ذَهَبْتُ); using لَا for "didn't"; reading سَـ as part of the verb.
- **Examples:** سَأُسَافِر خِلَال الصَّيْف. · لَن أَذْهَب الْيَوْم. · لَم أَذْهَب إِلَى السُّوق.
- **Deck:** Top words (سَوْفَ, لَن, لَم, قَد, لَقَد) and Top verbs.

### 4. `this-that`: this, that, someone, nothing (after `adjectives`)
- **Teaches:** ذٰلِكَ / تِلْكَ next to هٰذَا / هٰذِهِ; demonstrative + noun with ال (هٰذَا الْكِتَاب "this book" vs هٰذَا كِتَاب "this is a book"); هٰؤُلَاءِ for people; the indefinites أَحَد, شَيْء, لَا أَحَد, لَا شَيْء, كُلّ, بَعْض, نَفْس. Relative الَّذِي / الَّتِي for recognition only, **after** `respell.js` learns them (§6 of CORE-SOURCES).
- **English-speaker trap:** هٰذَا كِتَاب meant as "this book"; plural هٰؤُلَاءِ for things (use هٰذِهِ); "I don't want nothing" calques.
- **Examples:** هٰذِهِ سَيَّارَتِي. · ذٰلِكَ الرَّجُل أُسْتَاذ. · لَا أَحَد فِي الْمَكْتَب الْآن.
- **Deck:** Top pronouns.

### 5. `reacting`: oh my God, congratulations, what a shame (after `greetings`, or at the end of stage 2)
- **Teaches:** the exclamations people really use, grouped by feeling (surprise يَا إِلٰهِي, مَعْقُول؟, وَاللّٰه؟; pain آه, آخ; sympathy يَا حَرَام; joy مَبْرُوك → اللّٰه يُبَارِك فِيكَ; wishes بِالتَّوْفِيق, يَا رَبّ, آمِين; warnings اِنْتَبِه, النَّجْدَة); يَا before a name; the blessing replies as fixed pairs.
- **English-speaker trap:** carrying over "wow / oops"; missing the expected reply to مَبْرُوك or مَا شَاءَ اللّٰه; using أُفّ to an elder.
- **Examples:** يَا سَلَام! جَمِيل جِدًّا. · مَبْرُوك عَلَى الْعَمَل الْجَدِيد! — اللّٰه يُبَارِك فِيكَ. · اِنْتَبِه! السَّيَّارَة!
- **Deck:** Top interjections (every card has a dialogue), with Compliments for the blessing replies.
