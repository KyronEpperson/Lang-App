# cs-en · pronunciation conventions (settled in the cold review)

The Czech course follows the zh-en rules (`tools/content/zh/PRON-RULES.md`, sections A–D) without exception. This file records what was checked for cs and the few points a later writer could get wrong. Check with a copy of `tools/content/zh/pron_check.js` whose `D` points at this folder (result after the review: 865 pairs, 0 phrase conflicts, 1 allowed word pair, 1 allowed name).

## 1. Notation

- School IPA between slashes, American values: /oʊ/ not /əʊ/, /ɑː/ in «hot, doctor», /ɜːr/ in «work», r written wherever it is spelled (/ˈdɑːktər/), /æ/ in «can't, bank».
- Plain ASCII `g` (never ɡ), `r` (never ɹ), no flap symbol (/ˈwɔːtər/), no ɚ / ɝ.
- Long vowels always carry ː: /ɑː ɔː ɜː iː uː/. Diphthongs /eɪ aɪ ɔɪ oʊ aʊ/.
- Every pron contains at least one ˈ (the profile pattern requires it); a one-syllable content word is written /ˈmɪlk/.

## 2. Stress and weak forms

- ˈ on every content word of a sentence; function words are unstressed and weak: /ə/, /ði/ before a vowel and /ðə/ before a consonant, /tə/ before a consonant and /tu/ before a vowel, /fər/, /frəm/, /ət/, /kən/, /wəz/, /wər/, /həz/, /həd/, /wəd/, /wəl/ (unstressed «will»: «I think it will rain» /ɪt wəl ˈreɪn/), /jʊr/, /ju/, /bɪn/.
- A preposition left at the end of a clause is strong but has no ˈ. Negatives and contracted negatives are stressed (/ˈdoʊnt/, /ˈkænt/).
- «past» is the one word with two legitimate forms: preposition in clock times /pæst/, adjective /ˈpæst/ («in the past year»).
- ˌ only inside long words, -teen numbers (/ˌθɜːrˈtiːn/), compound numbers and letter-by-letter abbreviations (/ˌbiː tiː ˈdʌbəljuː/).

## 3. Syllabic consonants and names

- Final syllabic l after a consonant and syllabic n after t d s z ʃ ʒ v are written without ə: /ˈpiːpl/, /prɪˈskrɪpʃn/, /ˈpeɪʃns/.
- Names keep ə: Martin /ˈmɑːrtən/. Czech names are written as the en-US voice says them: Lenka /ˈleŋkə/.

## 4. Numbers, letters, punctuation

- Numbers are transcribed as words. Written as digits, a compound number is one token («25» /ˌtwentiˈfaɪv/, «22» /ˌtwentiˈtuː/); written out with a hyphen it is two («twenty-two» /ˌtwenti ˈtuː/, «ninety-nine» /ˌnaɪnti ˈnaɪn/). Years are read in pairs («2019» /ˈtwenti ˌnaɪnˈtiːn/, «2020» /ˈtwenti ˈtwenti/).
- Letter W is /ˈdʌbəljuː/ in the deck, the lesson and in abbreviations (the same as in the other courses).
- Two sentences in one row are separated by ` ‖ `. No punctuation inside the slashes.

## 5. Same English, same pron

- The same English text carries the same pron everywhere (lint enforces it). Before adding a row, search the course for the sentence.
- Notes and explanations may quote single sounds (/θ/, /ɪ/, /ŋ/) without ˈ; only the pron field must satisfy the pattern.
