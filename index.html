Reading drill

Type the romanization. Hangul blocks stack consonant + vowel (+ final). Your level here is also used in sessions.

How a block is built

ㅎ + ㅏ + ㄴ = 한 (han). Vertical vowels (ㅏㅓㅣ) sit right of the consonant; horizontal vowels (ㅗㅜㅡ) sit below. A final consonant always goes at the bottom.

Privacy & terms

Short version: your learning data is yours, nothing is sold, there are no ads, and you can take it with you or delete it any time.

What's stored, and where

  • On your device (browser storage): your progress, settings, nickname and custom phrases. Clearing site data or switching devices removes it unless you sign in.
  • If you sign in with email: your email address and a copy of your progress, kept in our database (Supabase) so it syncs across devices. Only you can read your row.
  • Feedback board: anything you post is public to all users. Your name is shown only if you tick "Show my name". We also store the page you posted from, the app version and your browser type (never shown publicly) to help fix bugs.
  • Analytics: anonymous page-view counts via Vercel Web Analytics — no cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal profile.
  • Error reports: if the app crashes, a short technical error message is recorded (no progress data, no email) so it can be fixed.

Your controls

  • Back up / move: Progress → Backup → Share file or Copy export; Import on any device.
  • Sign out on a shared device: choose "remove my progress from this device".
  • Delete everything: Progress → Account → Delete my account. Removes your account and synced progress permanently; your feedback posts stay but are unlinked from you.

Board rules
